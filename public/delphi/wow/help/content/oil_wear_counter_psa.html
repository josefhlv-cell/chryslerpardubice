[1097]Oil wear counter

There is a counter for oil wear in the automatic gearbox. This counter must be updated after oil change or when new oil is added.

The oil wear counts in units with a maximum value of 32958.

When the counter has reached maximum value flashes the sport and snow indicator lights. There is also a parameter in data list 9 that indicates when it is time for oil change.

Change of the oil wear counter shall be performed:

  • For any addition of new oil (oil change, replacing the converter etc).

  • Replacement of the automatic gearbox.

Observe:

When new oil is added: Reduce 2750 units from the current value for each 0,5 litre (s) of new oil that is added.

At oil change: Reset counter (Input value 0)

Ex: The current value: 28000. Adding of 0,5 litre oil.

New value: 25250 (28000-2750).

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on.

  • Battery voltage over 12 V.

Performance:

  1. Choose function: New value for service interval data-oil deterioration.

  2. Write the new value in the input field.

  3. Press OK.

  4. A dialog box present: Operation succeeded/operation failed.

  5. Verify in data list 4 that the new value is stored.

  6. When oil change is performed shall also the parameter for date of oil change be updated. Choose function: Write transmission oil change date.
