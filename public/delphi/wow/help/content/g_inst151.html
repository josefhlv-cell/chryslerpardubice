[905] Lambda probe positioning test

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to check the positioning of the lambda probes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe positioning test.

  • Press and hold down the brake pedal.

  • Press the throttle pedal fully down.

  • The engine control unit increases the engine speed to about 1800 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 350°C.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”Syst. OK!/ Syst. not OK!”.

  • If ”Syst. not OK!” appears, check that the correct lambda probe is screwed into the correct exhaust pipe (lambda probe bank 1 in the right-hand exhaust pipe and lambda probe bank 2 in the left-hand exhaust pipe).
