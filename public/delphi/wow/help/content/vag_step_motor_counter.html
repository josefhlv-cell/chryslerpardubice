[1388]Resetting of the counter for the left/right stepper motor

The stepper motors for the electromechanical parkingbrake is integrated in the rear wheel brake caliper. When changing someone of the stepper motors, it is necessary to reset the counter for each stepper motor.

Observe:

It is necessary to reset the counters when the following operation is performed:

  • Change of stepper motor.

  • Change of rear wheel caliper with stepper motor.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on and engine off..

  • Battery voltage OK.

  • No fault codes stored, concerning components in the system.

Performance:

  • Choose the function: "Resetting of the counter for the left/right stepper motor".

  • Confirm with OK. A dialog box presents: Operation succeeded/failed.

  • Confirm with OK to exit the function.

  • Read and check in the datalist that the resetting has been performed.
