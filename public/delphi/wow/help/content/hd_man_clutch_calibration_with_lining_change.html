[1666]Calibrate clutch

This function must be run if the clutch has been replaced, or if repair work has been carried out on the clutch involving replacement of the plates.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Transmission in low range position (gear 1 to 4).

  3. press down the clutch pedal and hold it down for 3 seconds. repeat the procedure 2-3 times to confirm the clutch limit values.

  4. press "OK". The clutch is calibrated.

  5. Turn off the ignition and remove the key for 5 seconds.

  6. Insert the key and turn on the ignition again.

  7. The function is done.
