[1078]Longitudinal acceleration sensor shutoff

This basic setting is used to shut off the signal from the longitudinal acceleration sensor. This sensor is only fitted to four-wheel-drive cars with Haldex clutch. When the longitudinal acceleration sensor is shut off, the four-wheel-drive is disengaged and the vehicle behaves like a front-wheel-drive vehicle.

Note:

When the ignition is switched off and on again, the longitudinal acceleration sensor is activated and four-wheel drive is engaged.

If the engine stops and is restarted, four-wheel drive is engaged again.

Method:

  • Start the engine.

  • Choose the basic setting: Shutoff of longitudinal acceleration sensor.

  • A dialog box confirms that the operation was successful/unsuccessful.

  • Switch off the ignition.

  • Switch on the ignition to re-activate the four-wheel drive.
