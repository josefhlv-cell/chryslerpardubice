Longitudinal sensor calibration
This function is used to perform a longitudinal sensor calibration.
Automatic learning of steering angle position in control unit shall be performed at replacement of control unit or in case of other jobs that may affect the calibration positions.
The front wheels must stand straight ahead to ensure correct calibration.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on
  • The steering wheel stands straight and the front wheels point straight forward.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Follow the instructions.
  3. The function is done.
