[584]Replacement timing sensor and/or cam

This function is used to reset timing end-position.

Note:

Shall be performed after:

  • Cam replacement.

  • Replacement of timing sensor.

If this is not done, the control unit cannot estimate exact air and gasoline flow and the following consequences may occur:

  • Unmotivated diagnostics for all components that are related to the throttle body and throttle control's position may occur.

  • Very poor driveability.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
