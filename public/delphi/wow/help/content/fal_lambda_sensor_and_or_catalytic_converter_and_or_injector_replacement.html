[583]Change lambda sensor and/or catalytic converter and/or injectors

Note:

When self-adaptation parameters are reset, they are reset to original factory-set values in the control unit. This function is used when:

  • Replacing injectors.

  • Replacing catalytic converter.

  • Replacing lambda sensor.

This procedure contributes to fulfilment of governing emission requirements. If this is not done, the control unit cannot estimate exact air and gasoline flow and the following consequences may occur:

  • Possible that unmotivated diagnostics for all components that are related to the throttle body and throttle control's position may occur.

  • Very poor driveability.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on

Procedure:

  1. Select the function 'change lambda sensor and/or catalytic converter and/or injectors' and follow given instructions.
