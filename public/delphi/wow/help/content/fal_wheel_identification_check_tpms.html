[36]Check wheel identification

This function is used to check that identification codes for the newly learned wheel transmitters have been received correctly.

Note:

For a correct procedure, the vehicle must be driven at a speed of at least 20 km/h or more for at least 1 minute.

When the vehicle is stationary, it is normal that a few or all identification codes are considered to be received. Therefore the vehicle must be driven at a speed of 20 km/h so that the identification codes can be stored correctly in the control unit's memory.

After each check, turn the ignition off and on so that the control unit can store the received identification codes.

Test conditions:

  • Drive the vehicle at 20 km/h or faster for at least 1 minute.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
