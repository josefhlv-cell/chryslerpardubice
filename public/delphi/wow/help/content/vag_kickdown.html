[1375]Kickdown

This function is used to learn a new kickdown position.

Note:

The learning of a new kickdown position shall be performed when:

  • The acceleration pedal module has been replaced.

  • The engine control unit has been replaced.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 11,5 V.

  • No fault codes stored in the engine control unit.

  • Electrical consumers switched off.

Procedure:

  • Press down the accelerator pedal and keep it depressed.

  • Choose the function: Kickdown.

  • During the adaption, "Adaption running" shows in the diagnosis status field.

  • When the adaption has been performed, "Adaption OK" / "Adaption ERROR" is displayed.

  • The adaption is finished. Exit the function.

  • Switch off the ignition for at least 50 seconds to store the values.
