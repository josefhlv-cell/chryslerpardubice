Calibration of the continuously variable transmission oil pressure
This function is used after the gearbox or the control unit has been replaced.
Note:
During the calibration the idle speed increases. Engine speed returns to normal after completed calibration.
Test conditions:
  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.
  • handbrake applied.
Procedure:
  1. Turn off the ignition and wait for at least 30 seconds.
  2. Turn on the ignition and wait for at least 2 seconds.
  3. Start the engine and wait for at least 5 seconds.
  4. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
