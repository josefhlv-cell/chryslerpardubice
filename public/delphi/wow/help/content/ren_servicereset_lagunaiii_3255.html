Resetting service message
This function is used to manually turn off the service indicator.
Procedure:
  1. Engage a gear or select "D".
  2. Press and hold in the start/stop button for approx. 10 seconds to turn on the ignition.
  3. Wait until the service interval indicator turns off.
  4. Press repeatedly on button A or B until "Time for service" or "Service vehicle" is shown on the odometer's display.
  5. Press in and hold in button A or B for approx. 10 seconds until the new service interval is shown.
  6. Let up button A or B.
  7. Press in the start/stop button to turn off the ignition.
