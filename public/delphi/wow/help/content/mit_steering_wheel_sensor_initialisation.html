[52]Initiation of steering angle sensor

When disconnecting the battery the learned neutral position will be erased and the indication ASC OFF will turn on.

Fault code 83 will be generated.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Turn the steering wheel more than 8° to the right or left, then turn the steering wheel to neutral position with the wheels straight ahead.

  3. Perform initiation.

  4. Check that ACS OFF indication is off.

  5. Erase any fault codes.
