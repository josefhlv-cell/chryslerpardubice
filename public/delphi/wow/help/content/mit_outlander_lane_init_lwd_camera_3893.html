Calibration: Camera internal data
This function is used to initialise the windshield mounted camera for lane monitoring ((LDW).
Note:
When the camera-ECU is replaced, first an initiation must be performed and then an image adjustment.
Before the camera is initialised, check that the camera mounted surface on the windshield is free of scratches, cracks, contamination or rain drops and check that the camera-ECU is installed correctly.
At calibration, use a white paper that penetrate light, which is not lined or contaminated.
A hand torch, with a wide lighting range, should be used at calibration.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition off.
  • A white paper which penetrate light.
  • A hand torch with a wide lighting range.
Procedure:
  1. Ignition off.
  2. Position a white paper on the windshield glass (on the LDW camera mounted surface). Then shine a bright light directly to it.
    • No shadow should fall on the paper.
  3. First perform the function "Decalibration: Camera internal data".
  4. Then perform the function "Calibration: Camera internal data".
  5. The LDW indicator in the instrument should now flash.
  6. Wait for 10 seconds, then turn on the ignition.
    • The rain sensor will be initialised automatically during these 10 seconds.
  7. Read fault codes, if the fault codes B1952 or C1703 are stored, repeat the procedure from step 2.
  8. The function is done.
