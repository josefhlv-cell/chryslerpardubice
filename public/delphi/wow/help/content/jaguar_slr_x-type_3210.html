Reset service message
This function is used to perform a manual service reset.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition off.
  • The ignition is turned on acc. to procedure below.
Procedure:
  1. Insert the key in the ignition.
  2. Press and hold down the button "RESET".
  3. During the time that the button is held down, turn on the ignition without starting the engine.
  4. The "RESET"-button has to be let up exactly when ignition position II occurs (listen for the "click").
  5. Wait 1 second.
  6. Press and hold down the "TRIP"-button (located on the direction indicator lever).
  7. Wait 1 second.
  8. During the time that the "TRIP"-button is pressed down, press and hold down the "RESET"-button.
  9. Within 3 seconds, the warning text "RESETTING SERVICE MODE" is shown.
  10. Hold down both buttons until the message "SERVICE MODE RESET" is shown.
  11. Turn off the ignition.
