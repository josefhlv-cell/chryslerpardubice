[755]Activation ABS

Conditions:

- Speed on all wheels below 5km/h.

- If failure is detected on pump engine, valves orwarning-light circuit, activation is not allowed.

- Ignition ON

Description:

Pump engine:

Activation of the hydraulic pump engine.

Humming sound from the pump engine.

Traction STC: (Stability and Traction Control)

Clicking sound from the hydraulic valve.

Stability DSTC: (Dynamic Stability and Traction Control )

Clicking sound from the hydraulic valve.

In/Out -valves:

Clicking sound from the hydraulic valve.
