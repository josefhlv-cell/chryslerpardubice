[157]Reset safety lock or the overheating protection

This function is used to reset a safety lock or the overheating protection. Causes of the parking/auxiliary heater being locked may be the following:

  • Disconnection due to too low voltage.

  • Overheating protection has been activated.

  • Too little fuel in the tank.

  • Identification of a collision event.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
