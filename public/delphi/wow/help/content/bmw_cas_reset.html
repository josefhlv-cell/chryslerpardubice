[1687]System reset

After work has been carried out on the "Comfort Access System", and in particular on the door handle, functional disturbances may sometimes occur in the "Comfort Access System" (CAS). A CAS reset must then be performed.

On vehicles equipped with a "start/stop" button, the lights go out briefly to signal that a CAS reset has been performed.

Test conditions:

  • Engine off.

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Perform the CAS reset by pressing "OK".
