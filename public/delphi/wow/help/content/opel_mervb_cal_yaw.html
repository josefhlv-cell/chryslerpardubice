[483]Calibrate yaw angle sensor

This function is used to calibrate the yaw angle sensor.

Teach-in of yaw angle sensor after certain service jobs:

  • Replacement of electronic brake control module (EBCM).

  • Replacing acceleration sensor.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

  • Parking brake applied or gear selector in position P.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.
