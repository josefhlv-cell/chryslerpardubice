Fuel supply resetting
This function is used to manually reset the fuel cut-off that follows a crash.
Note:
When activating the fuel cut-off function (following a crash), the fuel supply is cut off and indicated at the instrument panel and the hazard warning lights are switched on.
To restore the correct operation of the fuel pump, the following procedure must be followed using the vehicle's turn indicator lever.
If the fuel supply isn't restored after this procedure, check if any other ECU has fault codes.
Possible actions if the check isn't OK.
  • Restore the wiring between the steering wheel interface and the body computer.
  • Replace the steering wheel interface.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition off.
Procedure:
  1. Ignition off.
  2. Ignition on.
  3. Activate the right turn indicator.
  4. Deactivate the right turn indicator.
  5. Activate the left turn indicator.
  6. Deactivate the left turn indicator.
  7. Activate the right turn indicator.
  8. Deactivate the right turn indicator.
  9. Activate the left turn indicator.
  10. Deactivate the left turn indicator.
  11. Ignition off.
