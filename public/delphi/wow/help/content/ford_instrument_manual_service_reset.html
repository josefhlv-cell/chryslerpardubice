[1623]Manual reset of service message

This function resets service values and turns off the service light in the instrument panel.

Note:

This is a general instruction for Ford.

Procedure:

  1. Close all doors.

  2. Turn on ignition.

  3. Press down brake and accelerator pedal at the same time and hold down.

  4. When reset of service is done, the service light goes off.

  5. Let up the pedals.

    • Function done.
