[665]Calibrate clutch

This function must be run if the clutch has been dismantled or if repairs have been carried out on the clutch without the plates being replaced.

Note:

If the ECU is to be replaced. Read and write down clutch wear (Parameter 34), under clutch wear check during the test, before replacing the ECU. This value shall be entered after ECU replacement in this function.

This function may only be run on vehicle without control unit GS.

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Engine off, ignition on.

  • Battery voltage above 20V.

Procedure:

  1. Select calibration of clutch without replacement of plates.

  2. A dialogue shows the test conditions, check these.

  3. A dialogue shows "Do you want to perform calibration". Press "Yes" to continue or "No" to cancel the function.

  4. A dialogue with "Has ECU been replaced" is shown. Press "Yes" to continue or "No" to go to step 6 in the function.

  5. A box appears where you enter the value read from clutch wear (parameter 34) done before the ECU replacement. Then press "OK".

  6. A dialogue shows "Let up clutch pedal". Press "OK".

  7. A dialogue shows "Press down clutch pedal". Press "OK".

  8. A dialogue shows "Calibration performed". Press "OK".

  9. A dialogue shows "Let up clutch pedal". Press "OK".

  10. Turn off the ignition. Press "OK". Wait 5 sec.

  11. Turn on the ignition. Press "OK".

  12. A dialogue shows "Function done". Press "OK".

  13. The function is done.
