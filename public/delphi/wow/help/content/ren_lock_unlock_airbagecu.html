[1506]Lock/Unlock computer

This function is used for all operation on the airbag system.

When this function is activated, all the triggerlines are blocked and the airbag warning light on the instrument panel lights up continuously (ignition on).

This function only prevents the trig signal to the squibs, the airbag system components shall still be handled carefully.

After a repair, the ECU must be unlocked by selecting the unlocking function.

Check status:

  • Read the status of "Computer locked by tool" in datalist.

  • If the status is "Yes" the computer is locked or new.

To lock the computer:

  • Choose the function: "Lock computer".

  • A dialog presents: Operation succeeded/ failed.

  • Check that the airbag warning light is turned ON.

  • Check that the status of "Computer locked by tool" is YES.

To unlock the computer:

  • Choose the function: "Unlock computer".

  • A dialog presents: Operation succeeded/ failed.

  • Check that the airbag warning light is turned OFF.

  • Check that the status of "Computer locked by tool" is NO.
