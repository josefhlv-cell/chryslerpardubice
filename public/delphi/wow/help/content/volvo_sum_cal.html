[1398]Calibration of Four-C

Description:

To have proper Four-C funktion after repair the adaptive data in suspension module (SUM) has to be calibrated when following parts have been exchanged:

Control Unit.

Shock absorber.

Position sensor rear wheel suspension.

The vehicle and the components must have room temperature before calibration.

Make sure that the vehicle is unloaded on a flat surface and parking brake released with wheels straight.

Ignition on.

Erase faultcodes after calibration if needed.

Note:

For cars with Four-C this calibration also calibrates the xenon position sensor.
