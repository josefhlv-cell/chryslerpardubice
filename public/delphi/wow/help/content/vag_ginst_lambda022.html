[1335]Lambda regulation, diagnosis

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • Used for system diagnosis of lambda regulation

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda regulation, diagnosis

  • For vehicles to 04.01:

    • Raise engine speed to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • For vehicles from 05.01:

    • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

    • The engine speed will raise by the engine control module to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • Read the correction value. Prescribed value: under 0.02.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the status field shows "Syst. ok / Sys. not ok"

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
