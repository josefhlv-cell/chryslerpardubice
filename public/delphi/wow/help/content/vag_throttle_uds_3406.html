Throttle valve adaptation
During basic setting, the throttle valve is adapted to the engine control unit.
The adaptation shall be done when:
  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.
  • the throttle valve has been removed or replaced.
  • the engine control unit has been replaced.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • coolant temperature between 10 and 95°C.
  • All power consumers disconnected.
  • Battery voltage above 11.5 V.
  • Accelerator pedal fully released.
  • No fault in the system.
Procedure:
  1. Select the function: "Throttle control unit".
  2. Adaptation starts and a dialogue box shows: "Test active".
  3. Once adaptation is complete, the display shows: "Test complete".
  4. Press "OK".
  5. Cycle the ignition according to presented instructions.
  6. Check that no trouble codes are stored in the engine control unit.
    • If adaptation fails or is aborted, "Incomplete" or "Aborted for safety reasons" is displayed. Check the test conditions and repair any faults.
