[1596]Basic setting of the climate control

When the climate control unit has been replaced or a new shifting motor has been installed, a basic setting of the climate control must be performed. During the basic setting, the control unit learns the end positions of the shifting motors.

Test prerequisites:

  • The battery voltage should be above 11,5 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Choose the function: "Climate regulation", press "OK".

  2. During the basic setting the shifting motors drive to their end position. The text "adapt" is presented and changes to "in order" when the function is ready.

  3. End the function. Switch the ignition OFF/ON, wait about 15 seconds for possible fault codes to be stored.

  4. Re-establish the communication and check if any fault codes have been stored.
