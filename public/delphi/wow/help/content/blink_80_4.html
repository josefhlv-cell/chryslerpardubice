[1405] Cancel service message Volvo m/02->

Manually cancelling service message.

- Ignition position I.

- Press and hold the trip meter zeroing button.

- Ignition position II, with in 2 seconds after you have pressed the trip meter zeroing button.

- Press the trip meter zeroing button until the original value has been restored.

- A general yellow lamp (information lamp) is lit in the instrumentation when the trip meter zeroing button is to be released.

- Release the trip meter zeroing button with in 4 seconds. The instrument will alert with a sound if the cancelling has succeded.

- If the trip meter already is cancelled, the trip meter zeroing button must be pressed in min. 10 seconds and for max. 14 secondes.

Attention! If the trip meter zeroing button not is released with in 4 secondes the cancelling will not succed.
