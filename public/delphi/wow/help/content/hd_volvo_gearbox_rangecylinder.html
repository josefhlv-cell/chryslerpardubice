[1627]Range cylinder test

The test is used to check that the range cylinder's solenoid valves are intact.

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • The air pressure in the transmission shall be 650-1,000 kPa.

  • Engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 20 V.

Procedure:

  1. Select function, press "OK".

  2. A dialogue with test conditions is shown, press "OK". A sound from the gearbox shall be heard when the gear engages.

  3. A dialogue with values from the test is shown.

  4. Press "OK" and exit the function.
