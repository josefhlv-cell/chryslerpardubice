Normalisation of trunk lid
This function is used to normalise the trunk lid, when it has lost its reference positions.
Note:
No persons may be within the trunk lid's working range.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Trunk lid open.
  • Battery voltage min 12 V.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. For successful adaptation, "Adaptation done" is shown, press "OK" to continue.
If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
