[762]Programming tire size

Conditions

To get the ECU the right vehicle speed signal it needs that the right tiredimension is programmed.

Tire groups:

Group 1:

185/65 R15 , 215/45 R17

Group 2:

195/65 R15 , 205/55 R16 , 205/50 R17, 225/45 R17 , 225/40 R18

Group 3:

195/60 R15

Group 4:

235/35 R19

Procedure

  • Chose Tire group and press the OK button to perform the programming

  • Exit diagnosis and turn ignition off.

  • Turn on ignition and enter diagnosis again

  • Check that the right tire group are programmed by selecting the Read programmed tire size and click OK
