[917] Lambda probe after catalytic converter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Battery voltage above 11.5V.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to ”P/N”.

  • If cruise control is fitted, it must be working properly.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter

  • Let the engine idle. Press and hold down the brake pedal.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Keep the brake pedal pressed down until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”B1-S2 OK/ B1-S2 not OK”.
