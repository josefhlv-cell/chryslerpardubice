[212]Parametering of windshield

When replacing windshield it is important to teach-in the rain and light sensor to obtain correct function of rain sensor.

  • The windshield must be dry and free from dust by the rain and light sensor.

  • The position "interval wiper" for windshield wipers may be activated during the programming sequence.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The new adaptation values are saved. A dialogue box shows if the operation was successful or failed.

  3. The function is done.

    • If the adaptation fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
