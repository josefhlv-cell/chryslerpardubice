[721]Level adjustment

Level adjustment to make the height level sensors operational is required if the following work has been carried out on the vehicle:

  • Height level sensor replacement.

  • Control unit replacement.

  • Cable harness replacement.

When adjusting the level, the height is measured with a tape measure which is hooked to the centre of the rim flange at the bottom and measured up to the fender's edge.

Test conditions:

  • Before level adjustment is performed the engine must be turned on and off so that the air suspension is adjusted.

  • Any stresses in the chassis are released by rolling the vehicle back and forth for a short time.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

  • Ignition on.

  • The vehicle must be standing on level ground.

  • The vehicle's current load must not be changed. Doors may not be opened and the engine may not be started. This may lead to incorrect settings in the chassis.

  • Tape measure to measure the height.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is complete.
