[71]Initiate component

This function should be used after a component has been replaced or in case of malfunction.

Note:

  • The ignition must not be turned off during adaptation.

  • If the adaptation is cancelled or fails, check fault codes and test conditions.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • battery voltage above 12 V.

  • Ambient temperature above 10 °C.

  • Gas pedal not affected during the adaptation.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. A dialogue box shows progress of the adaptation.

  3. The adaptation is finished when this can be read out from status in the dialogue box.

    • If the adaptation is cancelled or fails, check fault codes and test conditions.

  4. Exit the adaptation.

    • After a successful adaptation, a control unit reset shall be performed.
