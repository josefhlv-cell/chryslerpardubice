[1189] Warm climate simulation (compartment temperature 30°C)

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running

  • A/C on and AUTO mode

  • ECON off

  • Set left and right required temperature to 28°C

This activation simulates A/C inputs of warm climate. The simulated signals and their forced values:

  • Coolant temperature = 80°C

  • Ambient temperature = 40°C

  • Compartment temperature = 30°C

  • Evaporator temperature = 5°C

  • Left hand sun sensor = 1000 W/m²

  • Right hand sun sensor = 1000 W/m²

Note:

The blower should be at low speed but blowing max cold.
