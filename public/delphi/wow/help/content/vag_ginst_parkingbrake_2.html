[ 1353]Basic setting of the brake system

The electromechanical parkingbrake is integrated in the rear wheel brake caliper. The electrical signal is transfered to a mechanical movement by a step motor and a multistage gear device to press the brakepads against the brake disc. After service on the rear wheel brakes shall a basic setting of the brake system be performed.

Observe:

It is necessary to perform a basic setting of the brake system when following operation is performed:

  • Replacement of the ABS control unit.

  • Service of the rear wheel brakes.

  • Fault code: 1087 Basic setting not performed. Stored in the fault memory.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on and engine off.

  • Battery voltage OK.

  • Parking brake not applyed.

  • Brake pedal not depressed.

  • No fault codes concerning components in the system stored.

Performance:

  • Choose function: Basic setting of brake system.

  • Confirm with OK.

  • Rear wheel brakes closes and opens three times.

  • Read fault codes and check that no fault codes are present.
