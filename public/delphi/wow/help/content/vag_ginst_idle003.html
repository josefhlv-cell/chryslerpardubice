[1307]Idling check

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to check idling.

  • Idling are not adjustable.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Idling check.

  • Read off the engine speed signal. Prescribed value 640-900 rpm.
