[856] Lambda probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter.

  • Engine idling. Press and hold down the brake pedal.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

Required values at test: Lambda probe after catalytic converter 0.000-1.000 V (must vary by at least 2 %). Diagnostic status must change from ”Test on” to ”B1-S2 OK/ B1-S2 not OK”.
