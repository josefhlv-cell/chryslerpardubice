[47]Steering angle calibration

This function is used to calibrate the steering wheel angle sensor. Before calibrating, the wheels must be set.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

Procedure:

  1. Turn the steering wheel more than 10° to the left or right.

  2. Turn the steering wheel to neutral position, so that the wheels point straight ahead, tolerance +-5°.

  3. Press "OK" to start calibrating. Do not touch the steering wheel during calibration.
