[883]Secondary air system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature 5 to 95 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the secondary air system.

  • This diagnosis is initiated only when the lambda probes have been tested and are OK.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Secondary air system.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Let the engine idle until the tank ventilation system changes from ”Test on” to ”Syst OK!”. / Syst not OK!”.
