[1185] Cold climate simulation (cold engine)

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running

  • A/C on and AUTO mode

  • ECON off

  • Set left and right required temperature to 28°C

This activation simulates A/C inputs of cold climate. The simulated signals and their forced values:

  • Coolant temperature = -10°C

  • Ambient temperature = -10°C

  • Compartment temperature = -10°C

  • Evaporator temperature = -10°C

  • Left hand sun sensor = 0 W/m²

  • Right hand sun sensor = 0 W/m²

Note:

Air distribution should be screen and blower level 5.
