[1059] Charge pressure regulation

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • To check the charge pressure regulation. The charge pressure valve opens and closes alternately at ten-second intervals.

Procedure:

  • Choose the charge pressure regulation function.

  • Note in the relevant value column that the charge pressure valve is working.
