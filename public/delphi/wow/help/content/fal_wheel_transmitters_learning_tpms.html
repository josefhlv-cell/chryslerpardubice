[33]Programming (learning) wheel transmitter

This function is only a manual function and only the procedure is described here.

Note:

This procedure shall be run if one or several wheel transmitters have been replaced. If the vehicle is driven at a speed of at least 20 km/h for at least 5 minutes, programming (learning) of the wheel transmitters is automatic.

Test conditions:

  • Drive the vehicle at a speed of at least 20 km/h for at least 5 minutes.

Procedure:

  1. Drive the vehicle at a speed of at least 20 km/h for at least 5 minutes.
