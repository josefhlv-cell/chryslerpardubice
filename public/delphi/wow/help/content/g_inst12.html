[870] Secondary air system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • To check the secondary air system.

  • See readiness test

Perform a test of the secondary air system:

  • Choose the secondary air system function.

  • The secondary air system changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Let the engine idle until the secondary air system changes to ”System OK!/System not OK!”.
