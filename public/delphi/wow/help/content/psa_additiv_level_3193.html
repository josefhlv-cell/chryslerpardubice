Tank level, additive
Use this function to specify the level in the additive tank when the tank is refueled or the additive control unit is replaced. The tank level is entered in percent (%).
Test prerequisite:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • The additive tank / control unit connected and installed correctly.
  • No faults stored.
  • All electrical loads should be switched off.
  • Battery voltage above 12 Volts.
Performance:
  • Enter the desired additive tank level (%) in the input field, confirm with "OK".
  • A dialogue box confirms: Operation succeeded / Operation failed.
  • When the additive has been filled, the level indication should be 100%.
