[1058]Injection timing

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • To check the injection timing. The start of injection should be varied from early to late at intervals of 10 seconds.

Procedure:

  • Choose the injection timing function.

  • Check that the value of start of injection varies from 14-17° before TDC to 4-0° after TDC.
