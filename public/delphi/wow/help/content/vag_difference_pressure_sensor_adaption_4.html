[325]Basic setting of differential pressure sensor

This function is used to reset the differential pressure sensor's adaptation values. This should be done when:

  • Replacement or repair of the differential pressure sensor has been performed.

  • The particle filter has been replaced.

  • The engine control unit has been replaced.

The adaptation is a manual procedure that is performed in several steps.

Test conditions:

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Procedure:

  1. Finish communication with the car.

  2. Turn off the ignition for 40 seconds.

  3. Turn on the ignition for 5 seconds.

  4. Turn off the ignition for 40 seconds.

  5. Turn on the ignition for 5 seconds.

  6. Turn off the ignition for 40 seconds.

  7. Turn on the ignition for 5 seconds.

  8. Turn off the ignition for 40 seconds.

  9. Turn on the ignition for 5 seconds.

  10. Turn off the ignition for 40 seconds.

  11. Turn on the ignition, re-establish the communication with the car.

  12. Read out and erase any fault codes.

  13. The function is done.
