[709]Calibration of the BCM control unit

This function is used to calibrate:

  • BCM - Lateral accelerometer.

  • BCM - Pressure sensor.

  • BCM - Steering wheel angle sensor.

Note:

In order for the stability function to operate correctly following repair, the control unit (BCM) must be calibrated.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Brake pedal released.

  • Position the vehicle on a level surface with the wheels facing straight forward.

Procedure:

  1. Ignition on.

  2. Turn the steering wheel around 15° to the right or left and then back so that the wheels point straight forward.

  3. Select the desired calibration and follow the instructions.

    • All sensors must always be calibrated following repair.
