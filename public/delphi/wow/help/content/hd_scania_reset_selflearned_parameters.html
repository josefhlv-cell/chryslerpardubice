[1655]Reset self-learned parameters

The parameters that are reset are those for wheel speed, rolling circumference, and calibration against the tachograph's speed.

The self-learning parameters result in optimized braking independent of the vehicle's load conditions and road angle. The braking force is adapted during driving to give a better pedal feel. When the load changes, the retardation control's parameters are reset automatically.

If there has been a change of trailer, driving conditions have changed, or pedal feel is not good, a reset of the self-learning parameters can be done with this function. This results in faster adaptation to the new conditions.

Test condition:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No fault in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function: "Reset self-learning parameters", press "OK".

  2. A dialogue shows: "Operation success/failed", press "OK".

If the operation failed, check test conditions.
