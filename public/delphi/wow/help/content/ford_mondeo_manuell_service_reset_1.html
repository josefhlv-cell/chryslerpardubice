[125]Reset service message

This instruction describes manual reset of the control light for oil change.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Press down the gas pedal and brake pedal at the same time, and hold down the pedals for approx. 20 seconds.

  2. The oil change light flashes for approx. 5 seconds and then goes off, this confirms that the reset is done.

    • If the oil change light does not flash, the reset procedure must be repeated.
