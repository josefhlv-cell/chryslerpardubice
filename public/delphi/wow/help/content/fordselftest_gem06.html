[845]Self test

Do not activate vehicle switches during the test.

Ensure that following conditions are met:

  • Vehicle stationary.

  • Make shure all the doors, the hood and the luggage compartment lid are closed.

  • Windsheild wipers are OFF and parked.

  • All abstructions are removed from the windsheild.

  • The washer fluid reservoir must be more than half full.

  • Turn signals, parking lamps and hazard lamps are off.

  • Drivers belt is not buckled.

  • A/C must be turned off.

  • Vehicles with sport/economy switch might need to turn the switch on/off during test.
