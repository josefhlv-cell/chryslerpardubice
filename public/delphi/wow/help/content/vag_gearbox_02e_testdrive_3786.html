Test drive after basic setting
This function should be performed after replacing the mechatronic unit and a basic setting of the mechatronic unit has been done.
Note:
Test drive is performed while driving and no diagnostic tool needs to be connected.
During the test drive, the clutches grip points are learned and stored in the transmission mechatronic unit.
Test conditions:
  • Transmission temperature between 30 and 100 °C.
  • Stretch of road that meets the requirements for a test drive.
Procedure:
  1. Check that the transmission oil temperature is between 30 and 100 °C.
  2. Drive in tiptronic mode on 3rd or 5th gear, between 1200-3500 rpm for about 10 minutes.
  3. Drive in tiptronic mode on 4th or 6th gear, between 1200-3500 rpm for about 10 minutes.
  4. Drive in position D and do a hard braking followed by a full throttle acceleration.
  5. Check that the clutches engagement feels OK by driving in position D and let the gearbox automatically shift from 1st to 6th gear.
  6. Connect the diagnostic tool and check that no fault codes are stored in the transmission control unit.
  7. Function complete.
