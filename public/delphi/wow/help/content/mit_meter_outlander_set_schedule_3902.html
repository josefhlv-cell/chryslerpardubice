Setting a schedule
This function is a manual description of how to set the service schedule, if the service message has been reset by mistake.
Note:
If the next warning period is cancelled by mistake, the cancelled warning period can be restored by "Setting a schedule" once, which then returns to the previous schedule.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition off.
Procedure:
  1. Ignition off.
  2. By operating the information switch on the instrument, the warning period is displayed on the multi-information display.
  3. Press the instrument's information switch for 1,2 seconds or longer.
  4. The service indicator starts flashing.
  5. While the service reminder indicator flashes, press the instrument's information switch for 1,2 seconds or longer and 3 times consecutively.
  6. The service reminder indicator starts flashing, and the current schedule is displayed on the odometer display.
  7. While the current schedule is displayed, press the instrument's information switch for less than 1,2 seconds and 3 times consecutively.
  8. The instrument display is shifted to the schedule mode.
  9. When the information switch is pressed for less than 1,2 seconds, the schedule is shifted, and when the switch is pressed for 1,2 seconds or longer, the displayed schedule is set.
    1. Schedule, "JPN", "EU", "NAS", "GCC/EXP", "MMAL" and "SEVERE" can also be selected. However, the using shall be for "Normal" only.
  10. In the schedule selected in step 9, the warning period to the next time is displayed for 3 seconds.
