Initiating rain sensor
This function is used to adapt the rain sensor to the windshield. The initialization does not affect the low beam sensor's function.
An initiation of the rain sensor shall be performed:
  • After changing the windshield.
  • If the rain sensor's electronic part has been replaced.
  • If there are malfunctions of the rain sensor.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • No faults in the system.
  • Ambient temperature between 15 and 35 °C.
  • Windshield clean and undamaged in the area for the optic unit.
Procedure:
  • Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  • Function complete.
