[1312]Knock control

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C.

Note:

  • Used to testing knock sensors.

  • The test will be done when driving.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Knock control.

  • The status field change from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • The Diagnostic status field changes from "Test ON" to "Syst. OK" / "Syst. not OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
