[1334]Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 350 °C

Note:

  • Used to see the ageing factor of the lambda probe (oscillation response). If it is to high, the probe is worn out.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check.

  • For vehicles to 04.01:

    • Raise engine speed to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • For vehicles from 05.01:

    • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

    • The engine speed will raise by the engine control module to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • Read off the value columns. Required result: Period 0.5 to 2.5.

  • The diagnos status field change from "Test on" to "B1-S1 ok" / "B1-S1 not ok"

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
