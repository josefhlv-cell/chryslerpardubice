Regeneration of particle filter
With this function a request is sent to the engine control unit that a regeneration of the particle filter should be implemented.
Regeneration of the particle filter is normally operated by the engine control unit, but for example at city driving, the exhaust gas temperature often does not reach the temperature required for a regeneration to start. Then you can request that it starts with this function.
Note:
  • As the vehicle must be driven during regeneration, for safety reasons, the driver must be assisted by another (adult) person whom is handling the diagnostic equipment during the whole driving.
  • Particle filter regeneration is active only as long as the speed is above a threshold of 20 km/h. If this threshold falls below in more than 60 seconds during the regeneration, the regeneration will be canceled and must be restarted by this function again.
  • The driving must be performed at a moderately trafficked highroad or freeway.
Test conditions:
  • No faults in the system.
  • Fuel level at least 10 litres.
  • The engine is warm (coolant temperature > 60-70 °C).
  • Speed > 20 km/h.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Start the engine.
  3. Check that all test conditions are fulfilled.
  4. Now request a regeneration in the engine control unit.
  5. Drive the car for at least 20 minutes at a constant speed of at least 20 km/h.
  6. Regeneration of particle filter starts as soon as the engine is warm and the exhaust back pressure/differential pressure is high enough.
  7. The status of the regeneration can be seen in the data lists, where you can also read the status of regeneration, temperatures and pressure information.
