[104]Correction service message

Correction of service message is used to adjust CBS condition-based service in case of an incorrectly performed service reset.

The following service messages can be corrected depending on model and model year.

  • Engine oil.

  • Spark plug.

  • Diesel particle filter.

  • Brake fluid.

  • Brakes front.

  • Brakes rear.

  • Microfilter.

  • Vehicle check.

  • Vehicle inspection.

  • Emissions check.

The vehicle inspection and emissions check are country-specific and therefore do not apply to all countries. It is possible to activate or deactivate these service messages under the selection "Configuration vehicle inspection" and "Configuration emissions check". Note that emissions check shall only be activated in China.

Note:

The original value of CBS condition-based service will disappear during the correction.

When correcting service message for brakes front and brakes rear, the thickness of the brake pad should be measured. The brake pads' wear sensors are divided in different steps, if the entered value is not accepted by the control unit, availability factor 255%, it is necessary to repeat the function and increase or decrease the value of the brake pads until the value is accepted by the control unit and a correct availability factor is reached.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Correct time and date set in the car.

Performing correction service:

  1. Select the function for the service message to be corrected.

  2. Current availability factor and service counter are shown for the selected service.

  3. If current service data shall be corrected, press "Yes", if the function is to be cancelled, press "No".

  4. Enter the new availability factor for next service in % and press "OK". For brakes front and brakes rear, enter the value in millimetre.

  5. Enter the new value for the service counter (shall in normal cases be reduced by 1 from the earlier read-out value).

  6. The new availability factor and service counter are shown for the selected service.

Performing configuration vehicle inspection and emissions check:

  1. Current status is shown.

  2. Select if the function is to be activated or deactivated, and press "OK".

  3. The new status is shown. It may take a few minutes before the correct service values are shown in the car's display.

  4. If the function has been activated, then date for next service must be entered in the car, this is done manually and is described in item 5. No further actions are required if the function has been deactivated.

  5. Entering date for next service can take place in two ways depending on if the car features CID (Central information display) or not.

    • If the car is equipped with CID (Central information display), the date is entered the I-drive control, located in the centre console.

      1. Access the service menu and select the service for vehicle inspection or emissions check by pressing down the I-drive control.

      2. Turn the I-drive control to select the month and confirm by pressing down the I-drive control.

      3. Turn the I-drive control to select the year and confirm by pressing down the I-drive control.

      4. The new date for next service is now saved.

    • If the car is not equipped with CID (Central information display), the date is entered in the Combination instrument via the BC-button on the direction indicator lever.

      1. Ignition on and engine off. Press the rocker-switch on the direction indicator lever upwards or downwards until you see the menu "Service Information" in the combination instrument's display. Press once on the BC-button.

      2. Press the rocker-switch on the direction indicator lever upwards or downwards again until the service for vehicle inspection or emissions check is shown. Press once on the BC-button.

      3. Press the rocker-switch on the direction indicator lever upwards or downwards to set month, confirm by pressing once on the BC-button, then press rocker-switch on the direction indicator lever upwards or downwards again to set year, confirm by pressing once on the BC-button.

      4. The new date for next service is now saved.
