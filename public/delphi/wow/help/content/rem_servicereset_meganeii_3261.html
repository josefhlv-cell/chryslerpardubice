Resetting service message
This function is used to manually turn off the service indicator.
Procedure:
  1. Do NOT touch any pedal.
  2. Turn on the ignition. For cars with key card, press in and hold in the start/stop button for approx. 10 seconds.
  3. Press in button A repeatedly until the wrench symbol flashes and "Remaining driving distance to next service" is shown on the odometer's display.
  4. Press in and hold button B for approx. 10 seconds until the new service interval is shown.
  5. Let up button B.
  6. Turn off the ignition. For cars with key card, press in the start/stop button.
