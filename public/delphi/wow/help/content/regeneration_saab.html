[1123]Manual regeneration

Warning-Fire Hazard !

Run the regeneration process outdoor with no combustible material under or behind the vehicle.Regeneration creates very high exhaust temperatures.

Regeneration burns soot captured in the exhaust particle filter and takes about ten minutes to perform.

Put transmission in P (or N for a manual)

Pull park brake.

Start the car.

Regeneration can be started when the car has reached normal coolant temperature.

Do not touch any pedals during the process.

Engine speed will increase and injection is delayed to drastically increase exhaust temperature.

Regeneration can be restarted in next key ON cycle if aborted prematurely e.g. if pedal is touched by mistake.
