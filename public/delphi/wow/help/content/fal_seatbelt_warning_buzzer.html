[1552]Seat belt warning buzzer

With this function it is possible to activate or deactivate the seat belt alarm.

Warning!

You are about to perform a measure that affects the vehicle’s safety. When you disconnect the seat belt reminder system you may put the occupants safety at risk. If the belt is worn the chance of survival is double in the case of a crash.

What is a Seat Belt Reminder and how does it influence the Euro NCAP star rating?

A seat belt reminder system is a system alerting the driver by means of sound and visual indications when a seatbelt should be worn. The reminder signal should be loud and clear but not annoying. The target is to remind people, who accept the benefits of the seat belts, that they have not fastened their belt. The points awarded for systems that meet the protocol requirements are added to the overall points score which in turn generates the occupant protection star rating.

The manufacturer of this diagnostic tool takes no responsibility for any injuries resulting from a crash regardless of the reason.

Note:

  • It is possible to read the present status in datalist parameters.

Test prerequisities:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Press "OK" to start the function.

  2. Choose activate or deactivate in the list.

  3. Press "OK" to continue or "Cancel" to quit.
