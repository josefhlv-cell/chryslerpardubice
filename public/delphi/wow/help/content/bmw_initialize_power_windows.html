[1586]Calibrate window lifts

After replacing window lift motor or if the car has been without voltage, the car may warn for "Anti-trapping protection", since initiation of the window lift is required to learn its end-positions.

Window lifts front are controlled by "FRM" module and window lifts rear are controlled by "JBE" module.

Test condition:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. Window lifts front are initiated from "FRM2", (Foot room module). Window lifts rear are initiated from "JBE2", (Junction box).

  4. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
