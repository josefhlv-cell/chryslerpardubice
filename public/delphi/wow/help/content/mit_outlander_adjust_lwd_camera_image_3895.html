Calibration: Image adjustment
This function is used to perform an adjustment of the LDW camera input image.
Note:
The LDW camera image can not be adjusted under unfavourable conditions such as snowfall. In such a case, you can eliminate a warning message in the insturment by pressing the LDW switch for at least five seconds until the conditions improve (any fault codes will not be erased).
To recover the display, press the LDW switch for at least five seconds again.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on.
Procedure:
  1. Start the engine.
  2. Start the function "Calibration: Image adjustment".
  3. The LDW indicator in the instrument will flash until the conditions below are met.
  4. Drive the vehicle for at least five minutes cumulative at 40 km/h or more under the conditions below:
    • The ideal vehicle speed is 70 km/h or more. If you can drive the vehicle on a dry, stable and straight road at 70 km/h or more during day time, the image adjustment will be complete more quickly. Note! Follow the existing speed limits where the driving is performed.
    • Road and environmental conditions should be so that the lane division lines should be clear.
    • A cornering radius of the road should be 300 m or more.
  5. The LDW indicator should go out.
  6. Check fault codes. If fault code C1706 is stored, perform the function "Decalibration: Image adjustment" and then repeat this function again.
  7. The function is done.
