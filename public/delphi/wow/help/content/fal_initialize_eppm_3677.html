Initialize EPPM (Electronic pedestrian protection module)
This function is used to initiate (lock) EPPM after replacement, to permit development of EPPM. The function should only be performed if EPPM has been replaced.
Note:
If EPPM has been replaced, EPPM initialization procedure must be performed with the diagnostic tool to clear the fault code, B2769-00 EPPM unlocked - all deployment disabled, and give the module complete preparedness. Perform all steps in the sequence to successfully complete the procedures.
Negligence to follow these instructions may cause serious or fatal injuries.
  • Connect all previously disconnected components - except the battery.
  • If the vehicle is equipped with keyless ignition. Disconnect the 12-volt battery and wait two minutes before continuing, remove the EPPM fuse, connect the 12-volt battery, wait two minutes before proceeding. Cycle the ignition to "On", then connect the EPPM fuse. Wait two minutes before continuing.
  • If the vehicle is equipped with standard ignition key. Turn on the ignition and then connect the 12-volt battery, wait two minutes before continuing.
  • Connect the diagnostic tool.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on.
  • EPPM replaced.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
