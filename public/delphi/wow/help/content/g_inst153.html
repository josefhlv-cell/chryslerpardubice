[907] Lambda probe after catalytic converter, cylinder bank 2

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter, cylinder bank 2.

  • Press and hold down the brake pedal.

  • Press the throttle pedal fully down.

  • The engine control unit increases the engine speed to about 1800 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 350°C.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”B2-S2 OK/ B2-S2 not OK”.
