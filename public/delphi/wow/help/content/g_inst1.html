[850] Basic setting requirements

Cars 10.94 >

Preconditions for the test:

  • Ignition on.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • Used to check basic setting requirements.

  • The basic setting requirements must have been met when setting the idling contact.

Procedure:

  • Choose the basic setting requirements function.

  • Check in the operating condition column that the second digit from the left in the block of numerals is a one (idling contact closed).

  • Open the throttle slightly. The 1 must change to 0.

Always 1: Short to chassis.

Always 0: Open circuit or short to plus.

Quit the basic setting requirements function and continue with the idling contact.
