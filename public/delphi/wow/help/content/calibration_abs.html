[774]Calibration of ABS

Conditions:

- Only cars with DSTC. (Dynamic Stability and Traction Control)

- When ECU or stability sensor is changed. - Car stands unloaded on a flat surface.

Description:

- Brake pedal not pressed.

- Ignition On.

- Turn wheels 15 degrees left or right.

- Turn wheels straight forward.

- Start calibration.

- Proceed all calibrations in a sequence.
