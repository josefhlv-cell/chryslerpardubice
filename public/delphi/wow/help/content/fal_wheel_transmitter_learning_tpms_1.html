[37]Programming (learning) wheel transmitter

This function is used for programming new wheel sensors using magnetisation in the TPMS-system.

Note:

The wheels to be programmed must be fitted to the vehicle when programming takes place.

Inflate all the tires to a pressure of 3.7 bar and wait for at least one minute. When the tires have been inflated to 3.7 bar, do not drive the car to obtain the best result.

To obtain good transmission/reception it is best to position the tire with the valve as close to the ground as possible. The procedure may take up to 1 minute per tire. If no code is received during this time, the function finishes with a non-approved result.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

  • Activation tool for wheel sensors.

  • Tire pressure 3.7 bar.

  • Access to all wheels that are to be programmed.

Procedure:

  1. Install the new wheel transmitters/wheel sets, to be programmed to the vehicle.

  2. Inflate the tires to 3.7 bar and wait for at least one minute.

  3. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.

  4. After finished programming, restore to normal tire pressure.
