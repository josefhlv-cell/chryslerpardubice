[586]Changing air flow meter

This function is used to reset all correction values of learned diesel volume in relation to the signal that is read by the lambda sensor in the control unit.

Note:

Must be performed when:

  • Changing air flow meter.

This procedure contributes to fulfilment of governing emission standards.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
