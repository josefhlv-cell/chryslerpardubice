[954] Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda regulation.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter.

  • Maintain the engine speed at 2600 rpm.

  • Diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read the lambda probe 2, bank 1. Prescribed value: 0.1 till 0.95 V.

  • Read the lambda compensation value B1-S1/B1-S2. Prescribed value: under 0.02.

  • Diagnostic status must change from ”Test on” to ”System OK/ System not OK”.

On completion of basic setting, the AC compressor and carbon canister valve are activated automatically.
