[455]Regeneration of particle filter

In certain driving conditions, the particle filter can not be cleaned automatically, e.g., in continuous city driving. Then this function can be used to force a regeneration of the particle filter. The function cleans the filter from soot particles by increasing the exhaust temperature and thus burn off soot particles. Increase of the exhaust temperature takes place automatically during the regeneration procedure.

Note:

  • The exhaust temperature is very high during this procedure. Therefore, perform the regeneration outdoors, without connected exhaust hose. The regeneration procedure may take up to 40 minutes.

Regeneration in progress can be cancelled by:

  • Affecting brake pedal or clutch pedal.

  • Turning off engine.

  • Any test condition not being met.

Test conditions:

  • Engine idling.

  • Coolant temperature at least 80°C.

  • At least 1/4 tank level.

  • Gear in position P.

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Engine hood closed.

  • Follow the instructions exactly and in order to enable regeneration start.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The following instructions are shown and shall be followed to start regeneration:

    • Press down clutch + brake pedal.

    • Let up clutch + brake pedal.

    • Press down accelerator pedal all the way.

    • Let up accelerator pedal.

  3. At active regeneration the engine speed increases to approx. 1,500 rpm and the glow filament in the instrument starts to flash.

    • If regeneration does not start, turn off the car and check test conditions.

  4. When regeneration is done, the engine speed returns automatically to normal idle and status changes to "test finished".

  5. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
