[1011] Lambda probe before catalytic converter (aging)

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Exhaust temperature above 350 °C.

Note:

  • Used to see the aging of the lambda sond (oscillation time). If it is too high, the sond is worn out.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: lambda regulation, probe before catalytic converter (aging).

  • Keep the engine speed between 1800 and 2200 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read off the value columns. Required result: Oscillation time 0 to 1 seconds: diagnosis status must change from ”Test on” to B1-S1 OK!/ B1-S1 not OK!
