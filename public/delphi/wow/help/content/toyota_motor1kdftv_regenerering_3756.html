Regeneration of particle filter
This function is used to perform a regeneration. The function cleans the particle filter from particles by burning them off. In certain driving conditions, the particle filter can not be cleaned automatically, e.g., in continuous city driving. Then it may be necessary to perform a regeneration to avoid clogging of the particle filter.
Note:
  • No flammable materials near the exhaust pipe.
  • The engine speed will increase during regeneration and returns automatically to idle when regeneration has been completed.
  • Regeneration time varies depending on the stored volume in the particle filter. However, it usually takes about 15 minutes to complete the regeneration. Do not cancel regeneration unless it is absolutely necessary.
  • If regeneration must be canceled, press the gas pedal and race the engine to abort the process. If the regeneration is canceled, it must be performed again.
  • It may not be possible to perform the regeneration when the ambient temperature is extremely low or regeneration is performed at extremely high altitude. In this case, perform the regeneration indoors (with the exhaust pipe pointing towards the outside) or drive the vehicle at a speed of about 60 km/h (37 mph) or more for about 20 minutes to perform the regeneration.
  • Depending on various conditions, perhaps the regeneration can not be completed within 15 minutes. If 20 minutes passes, the system will stop the regeneration and the MIL lamp turns on. If this happens, disconnect the cable from the negative (-) battery terminal or remove the EFI fuse for 1 minute or more, so that the MIL lamp turns off, perform this function again.
Test prerequisite:
  • No faults stored.
  • Coolant temperature is 60°C (140°F) or more.
  • For manual gearbox: Move the gear lever to "N" and apply the parking brake.
  • For automatic gearbox: Move the gear lever to "P" and apply the parking brake.
Procedure:
  1. Erase any fault codes.
  2. Start the engine and drive until the coolant temperature is 60 °C (140 °F) or more.
  3. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  4. Regeneration finished, check whether there is a need to perform the function again.
