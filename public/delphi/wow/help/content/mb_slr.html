[1571]Reset service message

After a service of maintenance, this has to be confirmed by resetting the service message.

When the service message has been reset the following is carried out in the system:

  • The service interval number is increased with one for the actual service.

  • The calculations for the next service date are reset.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Choose the service which has been performed and press "OK".

  2. A dialogue box shows: "Operation failed" or "Operation succeed".

  3. Press "OK" to confirm.
