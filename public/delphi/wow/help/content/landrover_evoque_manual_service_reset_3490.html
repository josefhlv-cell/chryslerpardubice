Reset service message
This function is used to reset the service message.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on.
Procedure:
  1. Navigate to the last tab in the service menu on the instrument panel display.
  2. Release the navigation button.
  3. Press and hold the "TRIP" button.
  4. Press and hold the navigation button.
  5. Hold both buttons depressed for 10 seconds, the service message are reset.
  6. Turn off and on the ignition, and check that the message is gone.
  7. If the message hasn't disappeared, repeat the process.
