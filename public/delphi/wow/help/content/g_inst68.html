[1025]Lambda adaption, before catalytic converter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to see the lambda adaption value for the sond before the catalytic converter.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda adaption, before catalytic converter function.

  • Read off the value columns. Required results:

  • additive lambda adaption (bank1, sond1) –6 % to 6 % and multiplicative lambda adaption (bank1, sond1) –10 % to 10 %.

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
