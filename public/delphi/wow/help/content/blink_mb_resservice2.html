Reset ASSYST (oil change reset) only after changing the oil!

  • Turn the ignition key in position ”2”

  • In the first 4 sec after ignition in position ”2”.

    Press 2 times on the reset button for the odometer in 1 secThe indication for service request will now be activated during 10 sec.

  • In those 10 sec, put the ignition key in position ”0”.

    The service request is still indicating.

  • Press the reset button for the odometer and keep it depressed.

  • Put the ignition key in position ”2”.

  • Keep the resetbutton depressed (for about 10 sec), until the signal sounds and the new start driving distance is shown at the display.
