Regeneration of particle filter
This function is used to manually start a regeneration of the diesel particle filter, when the conditions for starting are correct.
Note:
Burning of soot particles (regeneration) shall be done outdoors. The particle filter gets very hot, leave the vehicle on a non-flammable ground surface. To avoid subsequent damage, only perform regeneration once! The burn process may take up to 40 minutes.
When the conditions are correct and regeneration starts, the engine speed will increase while the regeneration is going on. When the regeneration is complete, the engine speed will decrease again. Exit the function when the regeneration is complete.
After completed function, turn off the ignition for 60 seconds.
The regeneration will be terminated, if the function is cancelled before the regeneration is complete.
Test conditions:
  • Engine idling.
  • Transmission neutral (M/T), P (A/T).
  • Brake and clutch pedal not affected.
  • Do not touch the accelerator pedal during the test.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
