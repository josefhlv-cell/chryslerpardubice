Standard adjustment after replacing the lock solenoid parking lock
This function is used to perform a standard adjustment of the transmission after replacing of component.
Note:
It is important that the first filling of transmission oil have been performed.
Check the oil level in the transmission with a separate function.
Test conditions:
  • Engine idling.
  • Transmission oil temperature above 35°C.
  • Parking brake applied.
  • Climate system off.
  • Gear selector in position P.
  • Place the vehicle on a level surface.
  • Battery voltage above 12V.
  • Brake pedal depressed (for the entire procedure).
  • There should be free space around the car, as it may twitch when checking the gear positions.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Transmission oil temperature must be above 35°C, to be able to perform the function.
  3. Brake pedal depressed (for the entire procedure).
  4. While the function is in progress, the letter "F" flashes on the instrument panel.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the dialogue boxes.
  6. It may take up to 6 minutes to complete the adaptation.
  7. If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
