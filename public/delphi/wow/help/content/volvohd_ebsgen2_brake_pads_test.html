[1522]Brake pad thickness, test

The test is used to show how much of the pad thickness which is left on the brake pads.

Note:

After completing the test, the ignition key must be turned off and on again.

Test prerequisites:

  • Parking brake released.

  • Wheels chocked.

  • Engine speed 1000 rpm, or constant air supply (12 bar) to air drier.

Procedure:

  1. Parking brake should be released.

  2. Increase the air pressure in the vehicle.

  3. Apply and release the foot brake with the ignition key in the driving position.

  4. Activate the test to read out the remaining brake pad thickness. When there is 20 % brake pad material or less left, the pads are worn out and must be replaced.

  5. After completing the test, the ignition key must be turned off and on again.
