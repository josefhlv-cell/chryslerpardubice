[737]Change service interval, Engine oil

This function is used to change engine oil service intervals and thereby extinguish any service warnings.

Note:

The fuel grades for different countries are shown below:

X<500 ppm = Western Europe, the Czech Republic, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Slovakia, Japan, USA.

500 ppm< X < 3000 ppm = Armenia, Belarus, Estonia, Kazakhstan, Poland, Russia, Ukraine, Australia.

3000 ppm < X = North Africa, Middle East, South Africa.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Engine off.

  • Parking brake applied.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Check conditions.

  3. Select oil and fuel quality according to your country (see list).

  4. Select other service warning parameters or cancel function.

  5. Check the relevant service warning values.

  6. Enter the mileage until the next service warning on the display.

  7. Enter the months until the next service warning on the display.

  8. Enter the hours until the next service warning on the display.

  9. Function complete.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and try again.
