Pressurization of air suspension circuit
This function is used to pressurize the air suspension, after it has been depressurized.
Note:
Do not put the vehicle down on the wheels before the function is complete.
Each suspension is individually pressurized in several steps and is controlled via dialogue boxes in the function.
Between the steps shall the air bellows position be checked, so the membrane runs correctly over the piston.
The suspension system must be depressurized before using this function.
If "Operation failed" is shown, the system must be depressurized before this function runs again.
For further information please refer to the vehicle's service manual.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Battery voltage above 12 Volts.
  • The suspension system must be depressurized.
  • The function must be performed with the wheels hanging freely.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  2. Pressurization occurs first on the right air bellow and then the left air bellow.
  3. A dialogue box presents "Function complete"/"Operation failed".
  4. Disconnect the diagnostic tool after successful pressurization.
  5. Place the vehicle on the wheels.
  6. Start the engine.
  7. Press the button to lowering to the lowest position.
  8. Then press the button to raise to the highest position.
  9. Repeat steps 7 and 8 three times.
  10. Dríve the vehicle at a speed over 10 km/h, the suspension will return to normal vehicle height.
  11. Ready.
