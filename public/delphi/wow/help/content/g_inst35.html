[989] Lambda adaption

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to check the lambda adaption procedure.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda adaption function.

  • Read off the value columns. Prescribed value injection time 1.7-5.0 ms; additive lambda adaption –10 % to 10 %; multiplicative lambda adaption –8 % to 8 %; status of tank venting –adaption (instantaneous lambda adaption). TE not active (condition for lambda adaption not met).

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
