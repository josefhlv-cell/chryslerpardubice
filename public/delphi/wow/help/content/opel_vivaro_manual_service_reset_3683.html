Recall service message
This instruction describes how to manually turn off the service light.
Note:
The following description applies until model year 2011:
The performance must be done within 100 seconds after the ignition is turned on (do not start the engine).
Procedure:
  1. Turn on the ignition.
  2. With the accelerator pedal fully depressed, depress the brake pedal three times in sequence.
  3. Release the accelerator and brake pedal and wait for 10 seconds (with ignition on) until each control lamp goes off on the display.
  4. Turn the ignition off and on in quick sequence (eg. within 3 seconds).
If the control lamp doesn't goes out on the gearbox display, drive for at least 15 km and then turn off the ignition for at least 1 minute before the procedure is repeated.
Note:
The following description applies from model year 2011:
Procedure:
  1. Turn on the ignition.
  2. Select the display "Remaining distance to the next service" on the trip computer menu by pressing any of the buttons on the end of the wiper lever.
  3. Press and hold any of the buttons on the end of the wiper lever until distance to the next service is displayed continuously.
