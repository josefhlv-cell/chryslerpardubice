[1528]Air dryer resetting

Note:

The resetting of the air dryer is used to reset the parameters connected to the funktionality of the air dryer. This test must be carried out in the following case:

  • The air dryer warning for consumed desiccator container is activated.

  • Replacement of the desiccator container.

Test prerequisites:

  • The parking brake must be applied.

Procedure:

  1. Apply the parking brake.

  2. Perform the resetting.

  3. End the diagnosis! Then turn off the ignition and wait 10 seconds before turning the ignition back on.
