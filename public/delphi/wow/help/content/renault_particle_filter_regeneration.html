[1593]Particle filter regeneration

Note:

  • This regeneration of the particle filter causes a rise of the exhaust fume temperature and may cause smell and smoke.

  • This funktion sets the engine speed to 1500rpm.

  • When the coolant fluid reaches 80°C it is normal that noise occurs from the engine (transfer to specific injection).

  • To avoid engine rush make sure that the engine oil level isn`t above the maximum level.

  • If the level of engine oil surpasses the maximum level, start the regeneration outside the workshop, unless the extractor fan extractor fan can handle temperatures higher than 600 °C

  • The engine speed is fixed to 1500 rpm until you press the vehicle´s start button to turn off the engine.

  • At the end of the regeneration it is normal that the engine speed alters (transfer to normal injection and several EGR interruptions).

  • IN STATE OF EMERGENCY! The regeneration can be stopped by two brief pressings on the vehicles start button.

  • The process takes about 30 min (depending on the time it takes for the coolant fluid to reach 80°C).

Test prerequisites:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults in the system.

  • Change engine oil before starting the regeneration.

  • Make sure the engine oil dosn´t surpass the maximum level.

Procedure:

  1. Choose the function "Particle filter regeneration".

  2. The particle filter regeneration is finished when the temprature before and after the particle filter has been lower than 550°C for more than 3 minutes.
