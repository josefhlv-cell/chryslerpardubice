[1300]Diagnostics for the EGR system

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 70 °C

Note:

  • Used to test the EGR system.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Travel for short while (about 3-4 min). Keep the engine speed at about 1800-3600rpm.

  • Take your foot off the accelerator suddenly. This way a deceleration is simulated.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • The deceleration phase must last for 8 seconds or more.

  • Status field changes from "Test on" to "Syst. OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
