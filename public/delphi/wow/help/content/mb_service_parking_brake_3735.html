Service of the electronic parking brake
This function is used for service of the electronic parking brake.
Note:
Warning! Risk of crush injuries when service mode is activated.
Test conditions:
  • The vehicle must be positioned so that it cannot begin to roll when the parking brake is released.
  • The parking brake switch must not be activated, after the service mode has been activated.
  • Ignition must not be switched off during the service work.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function
  2. Follow the instructions in the program.
  • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
