Initialisation of injectors
This function is used to perform a reset of the adaptation memory, for the injectors.
Note:
In order to learn new adaptation values, the vehicle should be driven varied with the following conditions:
  • Coolant temperature above 80°C.
  • At least 10 speed decreases from 3600 rpm - 1400 rpm.
  • Reduce the speed with at least 20 km/h at every speed decrease.
  • Do not touch the pedals during the speed decreases.
Test prerequisites:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Battery voltage min 12 V.
Performance:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Select an injector to initialize.
  3. A dialogue box presents: "Do you want to initiate more injectors?".
  4. If "Yes", repeat from point 2.
  5. A dialogue box presents: "Exit and drive the vehicle varied".
