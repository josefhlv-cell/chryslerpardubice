Filling the air bellows
This function is used to refill the air suspension system's air bellows after replacement.
Test prerequisites:
  • Ignition on.
  • Battery charger connected to vehicle.
  • The vehicle must be on a vehicle lift, the wheels should hang freely at least 30 cm above the floor.
  • Compressed air lines connected.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  2. Function complete.
After completed function, a level adjustment may be required.
