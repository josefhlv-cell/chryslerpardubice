[1418] Cancel service lamp A3 m/97 - 00

If service is due will “serviceOEL” or “serviceINSP” be shown in place of the mileage during a few seconds after the ignition on. The message will flash during 60 seconds after the engine has been started.

Cancelling

If inspection service “serviceINSP” has been arranged must “serviceINSP” and “serviceOEL” be cancelled.

  1. Ignition off

  2. Press and hold the trip meter zeroing button

  3. Ignition on

  4. Release the trip meter zeroing button

  5. Pull and hold the instrument clock adjuster knob for approx. 2 seconds

  6. Present service is now cancelled

  7. To cancel next service indicator press the trip meter zeroing button, and then continue from step number 5. (Depending of instrument version will ”---” or ”serviceINSP” be shown after step 7)

  8. Ignition off

  9. Ignition on, check display

  10. Ignition off
