[1529]Air dryer status

Note:

Using this status test it is possible to check the following data for the air dryer:

  1. Desiccator container, consumed quantity

    • 0% New filter installed, 100% Desiccator consumed.

  2. Status, the air dryer warning for consumed desiccator container.

    • Warning activated / not activated.

  3. Compressor, pumped amount of air

    • The unit displayed is volume in cubic metres.

  4. Compressor, degree of engagement.

    • Guidelines for normal compressor use. Drive type Degree of engagement:

    • Long distance 10-25%

    • Plant 15-30%

    • Distribution 20-35%

Test prerequisites:

  • The parking brake must be applied.

Procedure:

  1. Apply the parking brake.

  2. Perform the test.

  3. End the diagnosis! Then turn off the ignition and wait 10 seconds before turning the ignition back on.
