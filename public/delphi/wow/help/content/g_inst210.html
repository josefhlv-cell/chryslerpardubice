[970]Lambda probe after catalytic converter, readiness test

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter. The fuel/air mixture is enriched and the lambda probe voltage is observed. The probe voltage must rise.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter, readiness test.

  • Diagnosis status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Observe the lambda probe before catalytic converter value. The value must rise.

  • Let the engine idle until diagnosis status changes from ”Test on” to ”B1-S2 OK / B1-S2 not OK”.
