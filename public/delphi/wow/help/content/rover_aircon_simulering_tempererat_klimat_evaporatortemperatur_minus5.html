[1188] Temperate climate simulation (evaporator temperature -5°C)

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running

  • A/C on and AUTO mode

  • ECON off

  • Set left and right required temperature to 16°C

This activation simulates A/C inputs of temperate climate. The simulated signals and their forced values:

  • Coolant temperature = 65°C

  • Ambient temperature = 25°C

  • Compartment temperature = 27°C

  • Evaporator temperature = -5°C

  • Left hand sun sensor = 0 W/m²

  • Right hand sun sensor = 1000 W/m²

Note:

The compressor should be off.
