[1339]Lambda probe after catalytic converter, age check

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 200 °C.

Note:

  • Used to do an age check of the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Start the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter, age check.

  • Depress brake pedal.

  • The engine speed will raise and the status field will change from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • Maintain raised engine speed until the status field changes from "Test ON" to "B1-S2 OK" / "B1-S2 not OK".

  • Test may take several minutes.

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
