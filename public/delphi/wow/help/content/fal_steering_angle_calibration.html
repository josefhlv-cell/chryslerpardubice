[1607]Steering angle calibration

Steering angle calibration is performed in two steps. First the present calibration value is erased Then new calibration is performed and a new calibration value is saved.

Note:

At steering angle calibration the front wheels must stand in direction straight ahead to ensure correct calibration.

Test conditions:

  • Place the vehicle on a level surface.

  • The vehicle must be at a standstill.

  • Turn the steering wheel to centre position with the wheels straight ahead.

  • Do not turn the steering wheel during the calibration process.

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Select function "Steering angle reset", press "OK".

  2. Follow the instructions.

  3. Select function "Steering angle calibration", press "OK".

  4. Follow the instructions.
