[679]Replace oil accumulator gearbox

The function resets certain parameters in the controller that relate to the component being replaced.

The function must be performed if the oil accumulator for the gearbox is replaced.

Note:

Perform the following functions in the following order!

  • Before replacing the oil accumulator gearbox:

    1. Accumulator depressurisation.

  • After replacement of the oil accumulator gearbox:

    1. Replace the oil accumulator gearbox.

    2. Bleeding clutch.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Stop&Start function disabled.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
