[972] Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to see the aging factor of the lambda sond (oscillation response). If it is too high, the sond is worn out.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check.

  • Rev. up twice to over 5,000 rpm

  • Keep the engine speed between 1500 and 3900 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read the value columns. Required result: Lambda probe before catalysator, age check max. 1 s : diagnosis status must change from ”Test on” to B1-S1 OK!/ B1-S1 not OK!
