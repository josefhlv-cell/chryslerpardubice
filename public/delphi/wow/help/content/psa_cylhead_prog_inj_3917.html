Cylinder head replacement or fuel injector programming
This function is used to reset the adaptation memory after cylinder head replacement or after fuel injector programming.
Note:
  • The engine needs to idle during the process.
  • The function may take up to 5 minutes to run.
Test prerequisites:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Engine temperature above 70 °C.
  • Fuel temperature above 30 °C.
  • Disconnect the power consumers, climate control and fan.
  • Do not touch any pedals during the process.
Performance:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Start the engine and press "OK".
  3. Press "OK" when the temperature exceeds 70 °C.
  4. The function may take up to 5 minutes to run.
  5. A dialogue box shows the result.
  6. If the function fails, check the test prerequisites and repair possible fault codes.
