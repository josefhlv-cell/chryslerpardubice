Resetting service message
This function is used to manually turn off the service indicator.
Procedure:
  1. Turn on the ignition.
  2. Press repeatedly on button A until the wrench symbol is shown on the odometer's display.
  3. Press and hold in button A until the wrench symbol flashes and "Remaining driving distance to next service" is shown on the odometer's display.
  4. The new service interval flashes for 2 seconds and then continues to illuminate.
  5. Turn off the ignition.
