[927]Lambda probe before catalytic converter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to ”P/N”.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe before the catalytic converter.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter

  • Keep the engine speed between 2200 and 2800 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 352 °C.

  • Read the oscillation time of the lambda probe before the catalytic converter. Prescribed value max. 2.2 seconds.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”B1-S1 OK/ B1-S1 not OK”.
