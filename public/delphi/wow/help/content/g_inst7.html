[1027] Lambda regulation

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • Used for troubleshooting, by activating and deactivating the lambda regulation.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda regulation function.

  • It is now possible to alternate between lambda regulation activated or deactivated (Off or On in the lambda regulation status field) with the arrows above the value lines.

  • On completion of basic setting, lambda regulation is activated automatically.
