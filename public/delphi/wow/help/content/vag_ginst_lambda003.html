[1316]Fuel supply system and constant Lambda probe before catalytic converter diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • This diagnosis is initiated only when the lambda probes and activated charcoal filter system have been tested and are OK.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Engine speed 1400-1800 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Test may take several minutes.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnosis status field shows ”Syst. OK”.

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
