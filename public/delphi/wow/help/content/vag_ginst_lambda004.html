[1317]Lambda probe ageing diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • This diagnosis is initiated only when the catalytic converter temperature is above 350°C and the lambda values in data list 10 are "111".

    Significance of 3 digit number block in data list 10.

    xx1 Lambda regulation active

    x1x Lambda probe operationally ready

    1xx Lambda probe heating on

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Engine speed 1800-2200 rpm.

  • Keep the catalytic converter temperature above 350°C.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Check cycle duration, maximum 1.0s.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnosis status field shows ”Syst. OK”.

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
