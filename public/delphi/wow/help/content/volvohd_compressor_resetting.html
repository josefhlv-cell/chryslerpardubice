[1530]Compressor resetting

Note:

The resetting of the compressor is used to reset the parameters connected to the air volume pumped by the compressor. This test must be carried out in the following case:

  • Replacing of the compressor.

Test prerequisites:

  • The parking brake must be applied.

Procedure:

  1. Apply the parking brake.

  2. Activate the funktion, perform the resetting.

  3. End the diagnosis! Then turn off the ignition and wait 10 seconds before turning the ignition back on.
