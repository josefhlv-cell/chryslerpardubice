Resetting service message
This function is used to manually turn off the service indicator.
Note:
  • Do NOT touch any pedal.
  • For some engine versions, the engine may need to be running for the reset to work correctly.
Procedure:
  1. Turn on the ignition/start the engine.
  2. Press in button A repeatedly until the wrench symbol flashes and "Remaining driving distance to next service" is shown on the odometer's display.
  3. Press in and hold in button A for at least 10 seconds to reset the service interval indicator. The display shall flash after 5 seconds and the new service interval is shown. It flashes for 2 seconds and then is on continuously when it has been saved.
  4. Let up button A.
  5. Turn off the ignition. For cars with key card, press in the start/stop button.
