[1176] Resetting tank level counter

Description:

After refilling additive to the additive tank, must resetting of the Tank level counter be done.

Conditions:

- Start the engine.

- Activate resetting

- Check that resetting has been correctly done: Lock so that the status on parameter ”Level in additive tank is full” change to ”Yes”.

- If the status not change to ”Yes” check that the additive tank is filled up to ”max”, and check if there is any fault codes that must be settled.

- Caution! After resetting tank level counter must the fault codes that may occur be settled and erased, so that textmessages and the mal function indicator lamp is turned of.
