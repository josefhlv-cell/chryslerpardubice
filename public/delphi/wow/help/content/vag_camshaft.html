[1257]Camshaft adjustment

The inlet camshaft setting is used for a function test. Under the test the camshaft setting runs to the end positions.

Test prerequisites:

  • Engine running.

  • Brake and accelerator pedal depressed under the test.

  • The coolant temperature should be at least 80 °C.

Procedure:

  • Start the engine.

  • Choose the function: "Camshaft adjustment".

  • Press the brake pedal and keep it pressed down.

  • Press the accelerator pedal to full throttle. The engine speed is now increasing.

  • Keep the pedals pressed down while the test is performed.

  • During the test, the inlet camshaft setting runs between minimum and maximum positions in 2-3 intervals, which changes into a permanent position.

  • When the test has been performed "System OK" is displayed in the diagnosis field.

  • Release the brake and accelerator pedal.

  • Exit the function.
