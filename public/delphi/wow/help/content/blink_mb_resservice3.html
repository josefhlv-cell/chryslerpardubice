[1414] Resetting ASSYST efter an oil change on intrument cluster

Reset ASSYST (oil change reset) only after changing the oil!

  • Turn the ignition key into position “circuit 15R ON”

  • Press the system selection pushbutton until the multifunction display shows the default image (total and trip recorder). This is only necessary if the default image is not yet displayed.

  • Press the scroll forward/back pushbutton until the service symbol and the current remaining driven distance, remaining time or service exceeded is displayed.

  • Press the reset button at the instrument cluster for appoximate 3s, until the inquiry “Press reset button for 3 sec” appears in the multifunction display.

  • Press the reset button on the instrument cluster again within 5s.

  • The indication “Service interval was reset” appears.

  • The current “remaining distance” in km or miles appears in the multifunction display.
