Check the oil level in the transmission
This function is used to check the oil level.
Note:
It is important that the first filling of transmission oil have been performed.
Test conditions:
  • Engine idling.
  • Transmission oil temperature above 35°C.
  • Parking brake applied.
  • Climate system off.
  • Gear selector in position P.
  • Place the vehicle on a level surface.
  • Battery voltage above 12V.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Transmission oil temperature must be above 35°C, to be able to perform the function.
  3. While the function is in progress, the letter "F" flashes on the instrument panel.
  4. At command should the engine speed increase to between 2000 - 4000 rpm. Hold constant engine speed until the control unit decrease the engine speed automatically to idle.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the dialogue boxes.
  6. Note that the oil level should be measured at an oil temperature of 35-45°C or 65-75°C .
  7. If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
