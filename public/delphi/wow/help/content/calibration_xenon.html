[777]Calibration of xenon lamps

Conditions:

- Calibration needed when xenon lamp or sensor is changed.

- Flat surface.

- Unloaded car.

- Parking brake released.

Procedure:

- Ignition on.

- Xenon lights on.

- Calibrate.

- Erase fault codes.

- Check main light function.

- Check main light alignment and adjust if needed.
