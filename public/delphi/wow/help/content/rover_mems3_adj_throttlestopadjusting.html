[1205]Throttle stop adjustment

Turn screw clockwise to open throttle and decrease steps.

Turn screw anti-clockwise to close throttle and increase steps.

The pass range for approved positions are 15 to 25 steps.

Adjust the throttle to center of range.

If milage is less than 1000 miles then adjust the throttle to the top of the range.

After this proceed with the adjustment: Reset idle adaption.
