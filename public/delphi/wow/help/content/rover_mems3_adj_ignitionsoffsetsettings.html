[1200]Ignition offset settings

Caution:

Changing the ignition offset to a value which is not appropriate may damage the engine.

The engine control unit may be programmed to only accept a limited offset or no offset at all.

Ignition on.

Battery voltage min 11 V.

Set the ignition offset to retard or advance ignition.

The ignition may be retarted by up to six degrees and advanced by up to three degrees.
