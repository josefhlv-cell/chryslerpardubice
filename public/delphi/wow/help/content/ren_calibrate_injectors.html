[1134]Enter injector calibration data

Test conditions:

-Engine off

-Ignition on

-Check datalist "USING INJECTOR CODES" are displaying "YES", (Using injector codes).

Procedure:

-Enter the injector code with 6 uppercase characters

-E.g. 01ABCDEF (injector1 with data ABCDEF)

-Press enter

-Start again for the other codes otherwise finished

Important: Letters Q and J and figures 0 and 9 are not used.

Injector 1 is at the flywheel end.

(Injector data is to be found on the injector -> see picture)
