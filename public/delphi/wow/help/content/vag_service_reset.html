[1519]Recall service

In this function its possible to recall the indication for:

  • Service

  • Oil

  • Insp

Test condition:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  • Press OK to start the function.

  • Confirm with OK to recall the service, oil or insp indication .

  • A dialog box presents: Operation succeeded/failed. Confirm with OK.

    • If the text "Operation failed" is presented, check the connection or the model selection.

  • Exit function.
