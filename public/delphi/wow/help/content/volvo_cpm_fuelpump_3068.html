Activating the fuel pump
This function is used to fill the fuel line following repair of the heater unit.
Note:
To prevent the heater unit filling with petrol, the fuel line should not be connected to the heater when activating the fuel pump.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Battery voltage above 12 V. Battery charger recommended.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  • The function activates the fuel pump for around 30 seconds.
