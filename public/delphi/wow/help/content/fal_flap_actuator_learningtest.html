[339]Basic setting of air conditioning

If the control unit for the AC has been replaced or a new actuator has been installed, a basic setting of the AC must be performed. At basic setting, the control unit learns the actuators' end-positions and stores these.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • battery voltage above 12.5 V.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.
