[869] Lambda probe after catalytic converter, readiness test

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • Battery voltage above 11.5 V.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter, readiness test.

  • Press and hold down the brake pedal.

  • The engine control unit now increases the engine speed to about 1100-1400 rpm.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 400 °C.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read off the lambda probe after the catalytic converter. Prescribed value 0.00-1.00 V.

  • The diagnostic status field must change from ”Test on” to ”B1-S2 OK!. / B1-Se not OK!”.
