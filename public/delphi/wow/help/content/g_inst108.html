[857] Catalytic converter efficiency

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature 60 to 120 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • Lambda regulation and tank ventilation system OK.

Note:

  • Catalytic converter efficiency test.

Procedure:

  • Choose the catalytic converter efficiency function.

  • Engine speed 1500…3900 rpm.

  • The catalytic converter status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be at least 300 °C.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the catalytic converter efficiency diagnostic status shows ”Cat B1 OK! / Cat B1 not OK!”.

Required values at test: The catalytic converter efficiency must be at least 50 %.
