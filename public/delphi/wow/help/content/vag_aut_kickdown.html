[1255]Kickdown

This adaption is used to learn a new kickdown position.

Note:

A adaption of a new kickdown position shall be performed when:

  • The automatic gearbox control unit has been replaced.

  • The engine control unit has been replaced.

  • The gearbox has been dismounted/assembled.

  • The engine has been replaced.

  • The acceleration pedal module has been replaced or dismounted/assembled.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 11,5 V.

  • No fault codes stored in the engine control unit.

  • Electrical consumers switched off.

  • The accelerator pedal depressed under the adaption.

Procedure:

  1. Press down the accelerator pedal and keep it depressed.

  2. Choose the function: Kickdown.

  3. If the adaption is succeeded the text: "System in grundeinst..." shows in the adaption status field.

    • If the adaption unsucceeded the text: "Adaption ERROR" shows in the adaption status field.

  4. The adaption is finished. Exit the function.

  5. Switch off the ignition for at least 50 seconds to store the new value.
