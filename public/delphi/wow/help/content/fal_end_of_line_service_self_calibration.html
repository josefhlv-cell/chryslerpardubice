[678]End of line/service self-calibration

Note:

The function performs self-learning of values related to the gear choice and clutch engagement position, and checks that the movement of the clutch engagement position is correct.

We recommend that the "bleed clutch" function be performed before this function is performed.

Must be performed when replacing or during work that involves removal and replacement of potentiometers, solenoid valves, mechanical components and controllers.

The test takes around 5 minutes.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Stop&Start function disabled.

  • Hydraulic pressure more than 40 Bar.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
