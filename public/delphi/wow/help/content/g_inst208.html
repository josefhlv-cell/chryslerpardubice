[967]Catalytic converter diagnosis

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C

  • Intake air temperature below 60°C

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of catalytic converter efficiency.

  • This diagnosis is initiated only when the lambda probes have been tested and are OK.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Catalytic converter diagnosis.

  • Engine speed between 2300 and 2500 rpm.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be between 550 and 700°C.

  • The diagnosis status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain engine speed until the diagnosis status field changes from ”Test on” to ”Cat B1 OK! / Cat B1 not OK!”.
