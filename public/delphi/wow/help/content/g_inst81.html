[1040]Lambda regulation

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • Used for troubleshooting, by switching the lambda regulation on and off.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda regulation function.

  • It is now possible to alternate between lambda regulation on or off (Active or Inactive in the ”Lambda regulation status” field) with the arrows above the value columns.

  • On completion of basic setting, lambda regulation is activated automatically.
