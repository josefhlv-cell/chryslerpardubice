[1407] Clearing service message.

Saab 900 M1994-98

Ignition on

To restore the service message press the CLEAR-button for at least 8 sec. After 4 sec. the display indicate CLEARED and there will be a sound signal. After another 4 sec. the display indicate ”SERVICE” and there will be a sound signal.

The service interval will be count up and the day counter (only M1995) will be cleared.
