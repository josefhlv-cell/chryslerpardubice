Level calibration air suspension
This function is used to calibrate the air suspension.
There are three different load alternatives that determine the calibration heights.
Calibration of level sensors shall be performed when:
  • Control unit has been replaced.
  • Work has been done on level sensors or these have been replaced.
  • Calibration is defective.
Note:
The vehicle will move up and down when the air suspension is calibrated. Therefore, it is extremely important that no persons or equipment remain in the area around or underneath the vehicle, to prevent damage or personal injury.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on with external air supply connected or engine running.
  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.
  • On vehicles with a lifting axle, this must be lowered.
  • No faults in the system.
Procedure:
  1. Select the calibration function.
  2. Adjust upper level with the buttons in the program.
  3. Calibrate upper level.
  4. Adjust lowest level using the buttons in the program.
  5. Calibrate lowest level.
  6. Then the vehicle calibrates normal level automatically.
  7. Save the new values in the central data memory (CDS).
  8. Function done.
  9. Read out and erase intermittent error codes.
