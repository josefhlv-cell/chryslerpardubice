[891] Camshaft adjustment

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • For testing camshaft adjustment.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Camshaft adjustment.

  • Keep the engine speed above 1100 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!”.
