[1274]Checking the fuel pressure sensor

The fuel pressure sensor measures the current pressure in the high-pressure system and supplies a voltage signal to the engine control unit. The fuel pressure sensor should be checked when:

  • The engine runs in an irregular way or there is a bad engine performance.

Test prerequisite:

  • The engine on idle running.

  • No faults stored.

  • All electrical loads should be switched off.

  • The coolant temperature should be at least 80 °C.

Procedure:

  • Start the engine.

  • Choose the function: Fuel pressure sensor.

  • The fuel pressure regulator will change between "open" and "closed" during the test.

  • Fuel pressure set point:

    • During open fuel pressure regulator, approx. 600 bar.

    • During closed fuel pressure regulator, approx. 300 bar.

  • The test is finished. Exit the function.
