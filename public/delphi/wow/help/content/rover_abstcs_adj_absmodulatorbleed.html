[1181]ABS modulator bleed

1. Open the brake bleed nipple.

2. When start is pressed the ABS pump and valves will be activated approximately for 20 sec.

3. Repeatedly press and release the brake pedal while maintaining fluid level.

4. After approved bleeding keep the brake pedal firmly pressed and close the brake bleed nipple.

Note:

If it’s not possible to end the adjustment exit test mode or turn the ignition off.
