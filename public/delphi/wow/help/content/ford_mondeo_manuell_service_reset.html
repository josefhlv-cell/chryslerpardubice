[124]Reset service message

This instruction describes manual reset of the control light for oil change.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Close all doors.

  2. Press down the gas pedal and brake pedal at the same time, and hold down the pedals for at least 15 seconds.

  3. Press the button "OK" on the multi-function switch. The control light for oil change goes off, this confirms that reset is done.
