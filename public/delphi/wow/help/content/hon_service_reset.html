[664]Reset service message

After service is finished a service reset should be done. See list below what each service message includes. Service time is shown in both days and km.

Note:

Service alternative:

  • Service A = Quality of engine oil and oil filter.

  • Service B = Quality of brakes front and rear and adjustment of handbrake ( Note that on model Honda Accord 2008 diesel it is necessary to drain the fuel filter).

  • Service 2 = Quality of air filter and pollen filter (Also time remaining to inspection of drive belt).

  • Service 3 = Quality of oil in transmission.

  • Service 4 = Quality of following items (fuel filter, Poly V-belt, Spark plugs (Iridium), Idle motor and water pump).

  • Service 5 = Quality of coolant.

  • Service 7 = Quality of brake fluid.

  • Service 8 = Quality of air filter.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No faults in the system.

  • Transmission in neutral.

  • Parking brake applied.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.

  2. A dialogue box shows a scroll list.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
