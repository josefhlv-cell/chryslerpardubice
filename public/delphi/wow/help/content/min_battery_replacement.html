[269]Battery replacement

When a battery has been installed in the vehicle the function "Register battery replacement" shall be run. If registration of battery replacement does not take place the vehicle's power control will not work correctly. This may lead to malfunctions such as, e.g., reduction or deactivation of individual consumers in the vehicle.

Registration of battery replacement cannot be reset!

Note:

Should be used to register battery replacement with same capacity.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery charger connected to vehicle.

  • Battery replaced.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The presently registered battery capacity is shown, press "OK".

  3. Press "OK" to register battery replacement.

  4. The presently registered battery capacity is shown and the odometer reading for the four latest registered battery replacements. Press "OK" to exit the function.

  5. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
