[27]Automatic brake bleeding

This function is used to bleed the brake system.

Note:

Bleed all four wheels' brakes before performing automatic brake bleeding.

During the whole procedure (unless otherwise is stated), pump continuously with the brake pedal with regular pressing every other second.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Equipment for bleeding brakes connected.

  • Battery charger connected to vehicle.

  • Place the gear lever in park or neutral.

Procedure:

  1. Connect bleeding bottle to the car and set it to 30 psi.

  2. Perform basic bleeding before starting automatic brake bleeding.

  3. Connect the necessary equipment for bleeding the brakes.

  4. The procedure starts at the left front wheel.

  5. Start pumping continuously with the brake pedal with regular pressing every other second and start the function.

  6. Follow the instructions in the program.

  7. After finished function, perform basic bleeding on all four wheels.

    If the brake pedal feels spongy after finished bleeding, repeat the procedure.
