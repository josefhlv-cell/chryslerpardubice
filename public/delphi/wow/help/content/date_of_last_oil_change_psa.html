[831]Date of last oil change

After oil change it is possible to update the date of last oil change. The last oil change date is presented in data list number three.

Observe:

Write the date following: year – months – day.

Remove the two first figures in the year number.

Ex:

Date: 2005-10-12

Write: 051012

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on.

  • Battery voltage over 12 V.

Performance:

  1. Choose function: write transmission oil change date.

  2. Write the new value in the input field.

  3. Press OK.

  4. A dialog box present: Operation succeeded/operation failed.

  5. Verify in data list number three that the new value is stored.
