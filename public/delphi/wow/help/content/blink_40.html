[1403] Cancel service lamp S40/V40 m/99

S40/V40 m/99 is cancelled manually .

  1. Ignition on.

  2. Press and hold the trip meter zeroing button.

  3. Hold down the zeroing button until the service lamp start flashes (12 seconds)

  4. Release the trip meter reset button within four seconds. The instrument gives an audible signal on successful resetting. The service lamp goes out.
