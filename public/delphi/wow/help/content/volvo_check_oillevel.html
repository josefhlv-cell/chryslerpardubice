[647]Checking oil level

This function is used to check the oil level in the engine.

The signal is an analogue signal from the oil level sensor. The value shows percent of allowed level. Negative values indicate an oil level below, then oil must be filled. 0% corresponds to min. on the oil dipstick and 100% corresponds to max. on the oil dipstick .

Values above 100% indicate that the engine is overfilled with oil, then oil must be drained.

0 % = min. oil level

100 % = max. oil level

Note:

Recommended volume at oil change from manufacturer corresponds to a value of 70%

The oil level depends on the oil's temperature. For the read-out value to be reliable it is important to follow the instructions.

Test conditions:

  • Start the engine and warm up until the engine oil temperature is at least 60 °C.

  • Place the vehicle on a level surface at idle for at least 3 minutes.

  • Engine idling.

Procedure:

  1. When the test conditions are fulfilled, press the button OK to start read-out of the oil level.
