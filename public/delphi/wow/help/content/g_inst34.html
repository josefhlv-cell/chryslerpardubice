[988] Lambda probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda sond after the catalytic converter.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function. Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter

  • Maintain the engine speed at 2600 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read off the value columns. Required result: Lambda sond (bank1, sond2) 0.1 to 0.95 V; Lambda compensation value B1-S1/B1-S2 must be lower than 0.02; diagnosis status must change from ”Test on” to System OK!/System not OK!
