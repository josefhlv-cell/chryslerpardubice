[1106]P1300 Phonic wheel adaption

After this configuration fault code “P1300 Phonic wheel adaption” might appear. To reset this code disconnect the tester. Engine temperature has to be over 85°C and the engine speed must exceed 5500 rpm 3-5 times.

Note:

Just press the accelerator pedal until the engine speed is over 5500 rpm and then let the engine fall down to idling.
