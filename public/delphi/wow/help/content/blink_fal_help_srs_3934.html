Explanation guide to airbag abbreviations
Below is an explanation of how the SRS ECU is equipped and what types and number of airbags that are mounted in the vehicle.
Example of mounting, (2F-4P-4L-2T-1G-I).
xF - The number of front airbags.
xP - The number of pretensioners.
xL - The number of side airbags.
xT - The number of curtain airbags.
xG - TBD, information is missing.
I - Switch for disabling the passenger airbag.
S - Passenger detection system.
C - TBD, information is missing.
