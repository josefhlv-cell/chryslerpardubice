[1034]Lambda adaption

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • Used to carry out the lambda adaption procedure.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda adaption function.

  • Read off the value columns. Prescribed value injection time 1,5-3,0 ms; additive lambda adaption –12 % to 12 %; multiplicative lambda adaption –12 % to 12 %; status of tank venting -adaption (instantaneous lambda adaption), TE not active (conditions for lambda adaption not met).

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
