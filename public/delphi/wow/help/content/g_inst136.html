[888] Lambda probe before catalytic converter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe before the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter.

  • Keep the engine speed between 1800 and 2200 rpm.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be at least 350 °C.

  • The diagnostic status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”B1-S1 OK/ B1-S1 not OK”.

  • Read the oscillation curve of the lambda probe before the catalytic converter. Prescribed value max 1.99 seconds.
