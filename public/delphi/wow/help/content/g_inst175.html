[931]Checking the idling contact

Preconditions for the test:

  • Ignition on.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • Used to check the idling contact.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Checking the idling contact.

  • Read the operating condition. Prescribed value 0100 0010, where the second digit from the left in the block of digits corresponds to the idling contact (1=closed).

  • Open the throttle slightly. The 1 must change to 0.

Always 1: Short to chassis.

Always 0: Open circuit or short to plus.
