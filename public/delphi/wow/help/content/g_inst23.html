[976]Tank ventilation system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used for troubleshooting by letting the carbon canister be alternately closed or active (when active, about 220-900 seconds, open / about 70 seconds closed). By comparing the values shown with the carbon canister valve closed and open, you can assess the effect of the tank venting system.

Procedure:

  • Choose the tank venting function.

  • Now you can use the arrows above the value lines to alternate the carbon canister valve between closed and open.

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
