[1578]Clear counter of particle emission filter

With this function clearing of the distance counter for the particle emission filter can be performed.

Observera:

Clearing the distance counter for the particle emission filter must be done after replacing the particle emission filter.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Chose function: Clear counter particle emission filter, and follow the instructions.

If the operation failed check and repair any faults that may be in the system.
