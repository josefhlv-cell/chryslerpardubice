[1331]Lambda probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 400 °C

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe after catalytic converter

  • The status field will change from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • Read off the lambda probe after the catalytic converter. Prescribed value 0.00-1.00 V.

  • The diagnostic status field must change from "Test ON" to "B1-S2 OK" / "B1-S2 not OK".
