[1350]Lambda probe before catalytic converter (ageing).

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe before the catalytic converter.

Test prerequisites:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 600 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads should be switched off.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: 'Lambda probe before catalysator, age check'.

  • Depress brakepedal and then accelerator pedal.

  • The engine speed will raise and the diagnostic status changes from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • The procedure may take a few minutes.

  • Maintain engine speed until the diagnostic status changes from "Test ON" to "Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit the function.
