[1299]Climate control, basic setting.

Test condition:

  • Battery voltage above 11.5 V.

  • No faults stored.

  • Ignition on.

Note:

  • The basic setting must be performed when the control unit or a step motor is replaced.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Check the values of the step motors.

  • When both step motors are presenting 255, the basic setting is completed.

  • Exit the function.
