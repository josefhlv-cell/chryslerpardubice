[621]Adjusting DSC-unit

This function is used after changing DSC-sensor. This process adjusts pressure sensor, on/off valve, inlet valves, and fore-aft acceleration sensor.

Test conditions:

  • The DSC-unit must be coded.

  • The steering angle must be adjusted.

  • There may be no faults stored for pressure sensor, brake light switches, or valves (with exception of 5DD0 and 5DD2)

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

  • Pedals may not be activated during the function.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.

    • If adjustment cannot be performed, possible fault causes may be: Brake pedal was pressed down, leak in brake system, bleeding of brake system has not been performed, or bleeding device is still connected.

    • After adjusting the DSC-unit, perform bleeding of brakes, check for mix-up of brake lines, and initiation of tire pressure monitoring.
