Calibration of curtsey level ELC3
This function is used to perform a curtsey calibration.
Note:
The vehicle will move up and down when the air suspension is calibrated. It is therefore extremely important that no persons or equipment remain in the area around or underneath the vehicle, to prevent damage or injury.
Has the calibration failed, this can indicate that some level is out of range, or that there is to little difference between two calibrated levels. Check the driving level and calibrate again. Then turn off and on the start voltage.
To minimize fuel consumption and wear on the compressor, the curtsey level should be calibrated as high as possible.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on with external air supply connected, or engine running.
  • Measure from a flat surface to the lowest instep and calibrate the level so that statutory requirements and recommendations are met.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Set the correct level with raise/lower buttons.
  3. Press "OK" to start calibrating.
