[1692]Bed in handbrake shoes

After the handbrake shoes have been replaced, they must be properly set against the drums to ensure optimal results.

Note:

When running this function, it is important not to drive too fast or for too long, as this will damage the brake shoes and the braking effect may disappear completely due to overheating.

Test conditions:

  • Engine running with gear engaged.

  • Access to a stretch of road that fulfils the conditions set out below.

Procedure:

  1. Release the handbrake using the handbrake activation button.

  2. Select the "Bed in brakes" function and follow the instructions.

  3. The brake bedding-in light starts to flash when the function is activated.

  4. The vehicle must reach a speed of 15 km/h (+/- 3 km/h).

  5. At 15 km/h the handbrake activation button must be pressed. The warning light will now flash more quickly and the vehicle will slow down and feel heavy.

  6. After 100-200 m, the bedding-in phase is complete. To end it, press the handbrake activation button. The vehicle moves forward more easily.

  7. Bedding-in is complete.
