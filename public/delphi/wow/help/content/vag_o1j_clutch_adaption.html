[34]Adapting clutch

This function is used to adapt the clutch curve on CVT-transmissions. It shall be performed after:

  • Changing electronic control unit for the transmission.

  • Changing hydraulic control unit.

  • Changing input shaft.

  • Repair of input shaft.

  • Changing complete transmission.

Test conditions:

  • No fault stored in error memory.

  • ATF-temperature at least 60 °C.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Current ATF-temperature is presented, when the temperature is at least 60°C, press "OK".

    • A short test-drive may be needed to increase the temperature.

  3. Two data lists are presented with adaptation status for driving forward and reversing, respectively.

    • Move the gear selector to position "D"

    • Drive from standstill to slowly forward for approx. 20 metres and then brake to a standstill again.

    • Keep your foot on the brake for 10 seconds.

    • Move the gear selector to position "R".

    • Drive from standstill to slowly in reverse for approx. 20 metres and then brake to a standstill again.

    • Keep your foot on the brake for 10 seconds.

    • If adaptation succeeded the status now changes from "Adaptation in progress" to "Adaptation OK".

      (Sometimes the run-in has to be repeated up to 10 times for "Adaptation OK" to be shown.)

  4. Press "OK" to exit the function.
