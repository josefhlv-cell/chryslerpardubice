Particle filter regeneration
A particle filter regeneration is only necessary when the load state for the particle filter is over 40 % and no regeneration has been done automatically. This can occur when the vehicle has been driven at very low load. An ongoing regeneration is indicated in the instrument panel with the regeneration lamp on.
Note:
  • The load state for the particle filter is available in the data list.
  • If the load state is over 90 % a manual regeneration should not be proceeded due to fire risk in the particle filter.
  • The engine speed should be kept at approx. 1500 rpm during the regeneration.
  • The regeneration will take about 15 minutes to complete.
  • The regeneration will end if the ignition is switched off.
  • Exhaust temperature is very high during this procedure. Therefore, perform the regeneration outdoors, without connected exhaust hose.
Test prerequisites:
  • Engine on.
  • Engine at normal operating temperature.
Procedure:
  1. Start the regeneration function.
  2. Enter the required code number: 21295.
  3. Press OK.
  4. "Stored in memory" is presented. Confirm with OK.
  5. The regeneration lamp is activated on the instrument panel.
  6. Exit the function.
  7. Keep the engine speed at approx. 1500 rpm.
    • The regeneration will take about 15 minutes to complete.
  8. The regeneration is finished when the regeneration lamp is switched off.
