[1048]Lambda adaption

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to see the lambda adaption value.

  • Also used for troubleshooting by letting the carbon canister be alternately closed or active (when active, about 220-900 seconds open / approx 70 seconds closed).

  • The lambda adaption values for bank 1 and bank 2 are displayed during the basic setting procedure.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda adaption function.

  • Read off the value columns. Required results: additive lambda adaption (cylinder bank1 and 2) –10 % to 10 % and multiplicative lambda adaption (cylinder bank1 and 2) –10 % to 10 %.

  • Now you can also use the arrows above the value columns to alternate the carbon canister valve between closed and open.

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
