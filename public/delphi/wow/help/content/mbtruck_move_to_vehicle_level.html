[1542]Basic setting of vehicle level

After control module replacement the adaptive vehicle level must be programmed after the first calibration of the level sensors.

Basic setting of adaptive vehicle level only has to be done once.

Note:

When programming the vehicle will be moved up and down. Therefore it is extremely important that no persons or equipment remain in the area around or under the vehicle's frame to prevent crushing injuries!

Test condition:

  • Ignition on with external air supply connected, or engine running.

  • Vehicle must be without load.

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

  • All power consumers disconnected.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

    Basic setting is automatic and takes approx. 50 seconds.

    If the function fails, repair any faults in the control module and repeat the function.
