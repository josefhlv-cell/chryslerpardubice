[1337]Lambda probe before catalytic converter (ageing)

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 400 °C

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe before the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Depress brake pedal

  • The engine speed will raise by the engine control module to 1100-1400 rpm

  • Test may take several minutes

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on"

  • Maintain the engine speed until the status field changes from "Test on" to "B1-S1 OK/B1-S1 not OK"

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
