[10]Balance injectors

This function is used to activate the injectors to check correct fuel flow through the injector.

Note:

The injectors can only be opened or pulsed once per ignition cycle

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Fuel pressure tester connected.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.

After finished test, there may be fuel left in the inlet which delays engine start.
