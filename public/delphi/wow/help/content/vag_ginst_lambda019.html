[1332]Lambda regulation

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Coolant temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 352 °C.

Note:

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

  • Significance of three digit number block:

    • 1xx Lambda probe heating on.

    • x1x Lambda probe operationally ready.

    • xx1 Lambda regulation active.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • For vehicles to 04.01:

    • Raise engine speed to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • For vehicles from 05.01:

    • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

    • The engine speed will raise by the engine control module to 1800-2200 rpm.

  • Check cycle duration, target values:

    • Field 1: 111

    • Field 2: 110

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
