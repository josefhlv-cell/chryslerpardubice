[1556]Test of solenoid valves ABS

The test activates the solenoid valves to determine if they function correctly.

Note:

The test should not be stopped during the cycle.

Test prerequisites:

  • Parking brake released.

  • Brake pedal fully depressed.

  • Engine speed 1000 rpm or constant air supply to the air system.

Procedure:

  1. Depress the brake pedal and keep it there during the entire test.

  2. Start the activation.

  3. A dialog box will appear on the screen when the activation is completed.
