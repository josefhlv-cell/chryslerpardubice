Recall service
This function is used to perform a manual service reset.
Note:
Be careful not to execute the service reset again after completed service reset. If this is done, the next warning period will be cancelled.
If the next warning period is cancelled by mistake, the cancelled warning period can be restored by "Setting a schedule" once, which then returns to the previous schedule for service reset.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition off.
Procedure:
  1. Ignition off.
  2. By operating the information switch on the instrument, the warning period is displayed on the multi-information display.
  3. Press the instrument's information switch for 1,2 seconds or longer.
  4. The service indicator starts flashing.
  5. While the service indicator flashes, press the information switch once for less than 1,2 seconds.
  6. The service indicator change status to ON and "CLEAR" is displayed on the instrument for 3 seconds.
  7. After "CLEAR" is displayed for 3 seconds, the warning period to the next time is displayed.
