Adjusting level
This function is used to adjust the height level sensors. During the level adjustment, the height shall be measured with a measuring tape, hook the measuring tape at the center bottom of the rim edge and measure up to the fender edge.
Note:
Level adjustment to make the height level sensors operational is required if the following jobs have been done on the vehicle:
  • Change of height level sensors.
  • Replacement of the EHC control unit.
  • Replacement of the ICM control unit.
  • Replacement of the wiring harness.
  • Current fault memory registrations in the vehicle.
Test conditions:
  • Before level adjustment is performed the motor must be turned on and off so that the air suspension is adjusted.
  • Battery charger must be connected.
  • Ignition on.
  • In the ICM control unit must no internal errors be registrated.
  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.
  • Wheels in a straight-ahead position.
  • The vehicle's current load must not be changed. Doors must not be opened and the engine must not be started. This may lead to incorrect settings in the chassis and therefore an appreciable effect on stability and comfort.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Follow the instructions in the program.
  3. Function complete.
Note:
After the adjustment, the system will not begin to regulate immediately. It is therefore not unusual that the car is inclined, even after the adjustment.
