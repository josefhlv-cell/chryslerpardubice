[1562]Bleeding the brake system

This function is used when bleeding antilock brake systems (ABS). This is only necessary when at least one of the chambers in the brake fluid reservoir has been emptied, or when changing the brake fluid.

Note:

At bleeding or changing of brake fluid, approx 2 litres of brake fluid is required.

The bleeding should be done by the user in a sequence of several steps. Manual brake bleeding should be performed before this function.

To avoid overload of the ABS-pump, both wheels on the same axle should be bled simultaneously.

Test prerequisites:

  • Battery voltage higher than 12V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Correct brake fluid level.

Procedure:

  1. Clean the screwed nipples and connect the hoses for bleeding.

  2. Choose the funktion, press "OK".

  3. Open air bleeder nipples, press "OK" to continue.

  4. Depress and hold the brake pedal.

  5. Press "OK" to continue.

  6. A dialog box shows: "Bleeding in progress!". Hold the brake pedal depressed.

  7. Release the pedal. Press "OK" to continue.

  8. A dialog box present: "Bleeding in progress!".

  9. Press "OK" to continue.

  10. The steps 4 to 9 will be repeated 4 times in the sequence.

  11. Close the bleeder screwed nipples.

  12. Press "OK" to continue.

  13. A dialog box shows: "Operation succeeded".

  14. Add brake fluid.

  15. Repeat the steps between 2-14 at least 5 times, or until the desired result achieved.

  16. When the bleeding procedure is finished, check fault codes. If any faults are present, these shall be treated and the bleeding procedure should then be done again.
