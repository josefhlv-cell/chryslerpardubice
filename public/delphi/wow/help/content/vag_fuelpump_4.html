[1278]Fuel pump adaption

The basic setting adapts the fuel pump to the engine control unit. An adaption must be performed when:

  • The fuel pump has been removed or replaced.

  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.

Test prerequisities:

  • Engine running.

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • No faults stored.

Procedure:

  • Start the engine.

  • Choose the function: Electrical fuel pump.

  • During the adaption, "Adaption running" shows in the diagnosis status field.

  • When the adaption has been performed, "Adaption OK" / "Adaption ERROR" is displayed.

  • The basic setting is finished. Exit the function.

  • Switch off the ignition and wait at least 50 seconds to store the values.
