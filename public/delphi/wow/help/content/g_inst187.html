[944] Air mass meter

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • To check the air mass and injection period with unloaded engine at idling.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Air mass meter.

  • Read the injection period (mean). Prescribed value 1-4 ms.

  • Read the air mass meter. Prescribed value 3-5 g/s.

On completion of basic setting, the AC compressor and carbon canister valve are activated automatically.
