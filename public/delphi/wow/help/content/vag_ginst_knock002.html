[1313]Knock control

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • Intake air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to testing knock sensors.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Knock control.

  • Press the throttle pedal several times to at least 2500 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field changes between "Test OFF" and "Test ON" and after that "Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
