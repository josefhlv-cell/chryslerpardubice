Entering date
This function is used to enter the date when service or repair has been performed on the vehicle.
Note:
Write the date following: day - months - year.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Engine off.
  • Ignition on.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Enter 6 numbers for the date. E.g. 060701 (06 July 2001).
