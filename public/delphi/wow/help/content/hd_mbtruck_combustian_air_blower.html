[569]Checking combustion air fan

The test checks that the fan works.

Note:

The test can only be started when the fan has stopped.

It may continue for up to 3 min.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The fan's condition is checked.

  3. Select fan rpm.

  4. Perform the check.

  5. Press "OK" to finish.
