[1747]Regeneration of particle filter

This function is used to perform a regeneration. The function cleans the particle filter from particles by burning them off. In certain driving conditions, the particle filter can not be cleaned automatically, e.g., in continuous city driving. Then it may be necessary to perform a regeneration to avoid clogging of the particle filter.

Note:

Exhaust temperature is very high during this procedure. Therefore, perform the regeneration outdoors, without connected exhaust hose, with open hood. The procedure "Regeneration particle filter" may take up to 80 min.

Test conditions:

  • Engine on.

  • No faults in the system.

  • All power consumers disconnected.

  • Gear in P/N.

  • Engine temperature at least 70°C.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function "Regeneration particle filter", press "OK".

  2. Follow the instructions. Regeneration may take up to 80 min.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
