[306]Reset service message

Service indication is reset manually. Navigation takes place with steering wheel buttons and information is shown in SID (Saab Information Display).

Test conditions:

  • The ignition is turned on acc. to procedure below.

Procedure:

  1. Turn ignition key to position "ON".

  2. Select Settings with the Info-button, hold in "SET"-button until a beep is heard.

  3. Select "Time for service" with the "INFO"-button, hold in "SET"-button until a beep is heard.

  4. Select "YES" with the "INFO"-button.

  5. Cars with diesel engine: Keep the brake pedal depressed.

  6. A brief press on the "SET"-button now resets the service indication.

  7. Turn the ignition key to position "LOCK"
