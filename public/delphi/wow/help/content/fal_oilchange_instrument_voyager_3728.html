Resetting indicator oil change
This function resets indicator for oil change and is performed after an oil change.
Note:
This is a manual procedure, where you should press down the accelerator pedal and release it 3 times within 10 seconds.
The engine must not be running.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
  • Oil change in engine performed.
Procedure:
  1. Press down and release the accelerator pedal slowly 3 times within 10 seconds.
  2. Turn off the ignition.
  3. If the indicator for oil change turns on again when the engine is started, repeat the procedure.
