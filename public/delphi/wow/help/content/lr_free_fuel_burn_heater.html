[1638]Activations for auxiliary heater

The following is a list of activations for Auxiliary heater.

Note:

The auxiliary heater needs an ambient temperature lower than 5°C (+/-2°C) and the engine running to enable operation of the heater.

Test conditions:

  • Engine on.

Activations:

  • Warming up

    This function deactivates all temperature monitoring, except overheating monitoring (125°C).

    Max. activation time for this function is 8 minutes.

  • Coolant pump, heater

    Activation time is 30 seconds. This is also recommended max. time for the function.

  • Glow plug

    Activation time is 10 seconds. This is also recommended max. time for the function.

  • Combustion air fan

    Activation time is 30 seconds. This is also recommended max. time for the function.

  • Dosing pump

    Activation time is 30 seconds. This is also recommended max. time for the function.

    Warning: Repeated activation of the function may flood the heater.

    Then fuel may leak out through the heater's exhaust pipe.

  • Prime auxiliary heater

    This function is only intended for pumping up fuel after changing fuel line.

    Max. activation time is 40 seconds.

    Warning: Repeated activation of the function may flood the heater.

    Then fuel may leak out through the heater's exhaust pipe.
