Check the oil level in the transmission
This function is used to test vehicles without measuring stick. The test provides instruction for checking the transmission oil level, condition and for maintenance.
Note:
Instructions will be given for the removal of the fluid level plug and for monitoring of the flow.
Note that the engine should be started when you are instructed in the function.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on, engine off - the engine is started during the procedure.
  • Transmission oil temperature between 60°C and 70°C.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Start the engine.
  3. Follow the instructions in the program.
  4. Press down the brake pedal and move the gear selector slowly through all the gear positions.
  5. For vehicles with semi-automatic transmission, move the lever three times to position (+), and three times to position (-).
  6. Set the selector in position "P" with the engine running.
  7. Remove and dry the measuring stick, reinsert and make sure it is correctly inserted.
  8. Check the oil level at the stick.
  9. Low oil level may cause slippage, slowly clutch, damages to internal parts or may indicate leakage. Add fluid if necessary to the correct level.
  10. High oil level may cause uneven system pressure, hard/soft shifts or engagements, damages to the clutch discs, malfunction of the torque converter or that fluid runs out through the ventilation. Drain the fluid if necessary to the correct level.
