[232]Checking solenoid valves

This function is used to check every individual solenoid valve in the valve assemblies. Activate one valve at a time and listen for a hissing or clicking sound. The values are activated for a couple of seconds.

  • If no hissing or clicking sound is heard the valve may be defective or signal from the control module may be missing.

  • When a trailer is retrofitted it may be difficult to hear a hissing or clicking sound.

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Working pressure in air system.

  • Battery voltage above 20 V

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Select the valve to be activated.

  3. Listen for a hissing or clicking sound.

  4. Repeat the test for the other valves.

  5. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
