[968]Knock control

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • For testing knock sensors.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Knock control.

  • Keep the engine speed above 2600 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain engine speed above 2600 rpm until the diagnostic status field changes to ”Sys. OK/ Sys. not OK!”.
