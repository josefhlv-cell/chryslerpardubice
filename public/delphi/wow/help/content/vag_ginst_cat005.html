[1287]Catalytic converter diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • Intake air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the catalytic converter.

  • The diagnosis takes about 100 seconds. If no result is obtained after 150 seconds, change the engine speed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Catalytic converter diagnosis.

  • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

  • The engine speed will raise and the status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 352 °C.

  • Read the catalysator efficiency. Prescribed value at least 1,00.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from "Test on" to "Cat B1 OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
