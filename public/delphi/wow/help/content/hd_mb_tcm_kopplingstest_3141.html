Clutch test
This function is used to check that the control for the clutch cylinder works and to check the piston's movement between disengaged and engaged position.
At activation a ticking sound is heard from the clutch cylinder. At the end of the test, values are shown for the cylinder's stroke, current value shall be within the min./max. value.
Possible air malfunctions:
  • Leaking drain plug.
  • Malfunction of air supply to cylinder.
  • Dirt/oil in air channels.
  • Binding piston in cylinder.
  • Binding solenoid valve.
  • Leak in air connections.
Test conditions:
  • Parking brake applied.
  • Air pressure above 6 bar.
  • Gearshifting operation disengaged.
  • Battery voltage above 20V.
  • Oil temperature in transmission above 10 °C.
Procedure:
  1. Select function, press "OK".
  2. A dialogue box with test conditions is shown.
  3. Activate "Disengage / engage", a ticking sound should be heard and the sensor value for the piston movement should change.
  4. Press "OK", values for the cylinder's stroke are shown.
  5. The function is complete.
    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
