[736]Change service interval

This function is used to change service intervals and thereby extinguish any service warnings.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Engine off.

  • Parking brake applied.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Check conditions.

  3. Check the relevant values for the next service.

  4. Check the relevant values for the next service warning on the display.

  5. Select to change the service data or cancel the function.

  6. Enter the mileage until the next service.

  7. Enter the number of months until the next service.

  8. Enter the number of hours until the next service.

  9. Enter the mileage until the next service warning on the display.

  10. Enter the months until the next service warning on the display.

  11. Enter the hours until the next service warning on the display.

  12. Function complete.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and try again.
