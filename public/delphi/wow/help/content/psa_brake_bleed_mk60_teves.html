[632]Bleeding brake system

This function is used when bleeding the anti-lock brake system (ABS).

The bleeding method shall be performed after change or repair of the brake system's hydraulic circuit.

Note:

Bleeding consists of several steps to be performed by the user.

Manual bleeding shall be done before and after this function on all wheels.

Bleeding order for manual bleeding: Left front, right front, right rear, left rear.

This bleeding function shall be repeated at least 5 times or until the desired result is obtained.

NOTE, it is not necessary to bleed the rear brake calipers in the function (they are bled during manual phases).

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

  • A co-worker is needed to assist with the bleeding method.

  • Correct brake fluid level.

Procedure:

  1. Connect any bleeding equipment (not necessary).

  2. Perform manual bleeding of the system on all wheels.

  3. Start the function.

  4. Follow the instructions in the program.

  5. A dialogue box shows "Operation successful/ failed".

  6. Function done.

  7. Repeat this function from step 3 at least 5 times or until the desired result is obtained.

  8. Remove the bleeding equipment, if any is used, from the brake fluid reservoir.

  9. Now again perform manual bleeding of the system on all wheels acc. to the sequence order.

  10. After finished bleed procedure, check error codes. Take care of any malfunctions, if there are any, and then repeat the bleeding procedure.
