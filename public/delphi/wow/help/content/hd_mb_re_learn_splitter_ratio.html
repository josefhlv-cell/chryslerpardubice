[133]Basic setting of splitter ratio

This function is used to learn the splitter ratio again.

Note:

This function may only be run on vehicle without control unit "GS".

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Gear selector in position N.

  • Battery voltage above 20V.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. A dialogue shows the test conditions, check these.

  3. A dialogue with "Engage high split" is shown. Engage high split without pressing down the clutch and press "OK".

  4. A dialogue with "Engage low split" is shown. Enage low split without pressing down the clutch and press "OK".

  5. A dialogue with "Start engine" is shown. Start engine and press "OK".

  6. A dialogue with "Press down and let up clutch pedal" is shown.

  7. A dialogue with "Low split learned. Change to high split, then press down and let up clutch pedal" is shown.

  8. A dialogue box shows "Turn off ignition". Turn off ignition, press "OK".

  9. A dialogue box shows "Turn on ignition". Turn on the ignition, press "OK".

  10. A dialogue shows "Function done". Press "OK".

  11. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
