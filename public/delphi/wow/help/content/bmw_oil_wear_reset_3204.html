QMVH reset of oil wear
This function is used to perform a reset of oil wear.
The rear axle stabilizer torque distribution (QMVH) controls the extra control elements on the rear axle and distributes the drive torque targeted between the two wheels on the rear axle. In PR-situations the system also has the name "Dynamic Performance Control".
Note:
If the rear axle stabilizer torque distribution does not work the control/warning lights for DSC and X-Drive are turned on. If a malfunction occurs an associated symbol shows a check-control message in the LCD.
Test conditions:
  • Oil change completed.
  • Ignition on.
  • Battery voltage OK.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  2. Function complete.
