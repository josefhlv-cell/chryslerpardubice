Break-in of refrigerant compressor
This function activates the break-in procedure for the refrigerant compressor.
Break-in should only be performed once a new refrigerant compressor has been fitted.
Test conditions:
  • Engine idling.
  • The air conditioning should be sufficiently filled and operational.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. The break-in process starts and may last for up to 90 seconds.
  3. The "A/C" button will flash during the break-in process.
  4. The function is complete.
