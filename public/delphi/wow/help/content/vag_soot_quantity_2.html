[1619]Reset the adaptation values for particle filter

When replacing particle filter, the adaptation values for soot quantity and soot volume must be reset in the engine control unit.

Note:

  • After resetting the ignition shall be turned off for 30 seconds to store the new values.

Test condition:

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function: "Particle filter replaced".

  2. Confirm with "OK". A dialogue box shows: "Operation successful" or "Operation failed".

    • If the function failed, check test conditions.

  3. Confirm with "OK" to exit the function.

  4. Turn off the ignition for 30 seconds to store the new value.
