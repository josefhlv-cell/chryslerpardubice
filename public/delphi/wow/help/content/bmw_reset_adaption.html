[1689]Reset adaptation values

When the adaptation values are set to zero, the original values are restored in the ECU.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Select the "reset adaptation values" function and press "OK".

  2. Wait 5 seconds.

  3. Switch off the ignition, press "OK".

  4. Wait 15 seconds.

  5. Switch on the ignition and press "OK".

  6. Leave the ignition on for at least 10 seconds after the function is complete, then press "OK".
