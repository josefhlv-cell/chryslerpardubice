[1770]Reset service information after oil change

The engine control module contains a function that calculates contamination of the engine oil and recommends changing the same. If the counter is not reset after oil change, the count-up continues and results in a premature request for oil change.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Oil change in engine done.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Contamination counter is reset.

  3. The function is done.
