[35]Reset calculation of transmission oil's wear

This function shall be run after oil change on the automatic transmission.

Calculation of the transmission oil's service life is reset. After the oil change, you have to check the oil level in the automatic transmission with the function "Checking oil level in the automatic transmission".

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The transmission oil's present calculation is read out.

  3. In case of an oil change, the calculation shall always be reset.

  4. After resetting, the new calculation for the transmission oil is read out again.

  5. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
