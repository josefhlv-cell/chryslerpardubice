[1490]Resetting of the blocking protection for parking/additional heater.

Note:

The reasons why the parking/additional heater is locked can be the following:

  • Under-voltage cutout.

  • Activation of the overheating protection.

  • Lack of fuel.

Test prerequisites:

  • No fault codes in the automatic climate control unit.

  • Connect the battery charger.

  • The fuel level should not be less than 15 liters.

Procedure:

  1. Ignition on.

  2. Remove the fuse for the parking/additional heater.

  3. Wait for 5 seconds and then reassemble the fuse.

  4. The blocking protection is now reset.

  5. Start the parking/additional heater and let it be active for at least 3 minutes before shutting it off.
