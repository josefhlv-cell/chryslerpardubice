[1561]PWM valves clutch, test

The purpose of the test is to check that the control of the clutch cylinder works and to check for possible air leakage and the performance of the clutch.

Note:

The test must be carried out in the following order:

  1. Leak test clutch, PWM valves.

  2. Clutch performance, PWM valves.

Test prerequisites:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Air pressure in gearbox below 1000 kPa.

  • Engine off.

  • Battery voltage higher than 20 V.

Procedure:

  1. Choose the function: "Leak test clutch, PWM valves", press "OK".

  2. Check that value after test is between min and max.

  3. Choose the function: "Clutch performance, PWM valves", press "OK".

  4. Check that all clutch times are within the limits.

  5. Exit the test.
