[1346]Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probes after the catalytic converter.

  • The test will be done when driving.

Procedure:

  • Choose function: Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter.

  • The status field will change from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • The status will changes from "Test ON" to "Syst OK / Syst not OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
