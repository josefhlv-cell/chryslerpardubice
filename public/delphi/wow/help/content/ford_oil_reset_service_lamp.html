[96]Reset service message

This function resets service values and turns off the oil change light in the instrument panel.

Procedure:

  1. Turn on the ignition, and press down the gas pedal and brake pedal at the same time, hold down the pedals for approx. 15 seconds, until the oil change light starts to flash or the message "Service Oil Reset Complete" is shown in the information centre (if applicable).

  2. Let up the pedals.

  3. Check that the oil change light has turned off and that the service message in the information centre (if applicable) is not shown any longer.

    • If the oil change light still is on or the service message is still shown, turn off the ignition and repeat the procedure from step 1. If the light is off or the service message has disappeared, continue to step 4.

  4. Turn off the ignition for at least 2 seconds.

  5. Turn on the ignition and check that the oil change light is not on or the service message is shown.

  6. The function is done.
