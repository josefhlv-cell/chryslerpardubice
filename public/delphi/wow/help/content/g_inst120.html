[871] Lambda regulation, diagnosis

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used for system diagnosis of lambda regulation.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda regulation, diagnosis.

  • Press and hold down the brake pedal. Within a few seconds the engine control unit will raise the engine speed to 1100-1400 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field shows ”Sys. OK! / Sys. not OK!”.
