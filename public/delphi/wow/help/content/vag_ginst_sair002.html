[1355]Secondary air system diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • To check the secondary air system.

  • Under the diagnosis the secondary air pump starts for a few seconds.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Secondary air system.

  • Press the throttle pedal short to at least 2500 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to "Test on".

  • Let the engine idle until the diagnostic status changes from "Test on" to "Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
