[1170]Computer calibration

Test conditions:

-Engine on

-Ignition on

Explanation :

Status 0 = An uncalibrated or new computer will always be set to 0 by default.

Status 1 = K9K engine with manual gearbox or K4J and K4M with manual or automatic gearbox,fitted with 16-inch wheels or less.

Status 2 = K9K engine with automatic gearbox or F4R and F9Q with manual or automatic gearbox, fitted with 16-inch wheels or less.

Status 3 = K9K engine with manual gearbox or K4J and K4M with manual or automatic gearbox,fitted with 17-inch wheels or larger.

Status 4 = K9K engine with automatic gearbox or F4R and F9Q with manual or automatic gearbox, fitted with 17-inch wheels or larger.
