[590]Check the oil level in the transmission

Note:

The function must be run after any of the following jobs has been done:

  • After changing oil in the automatic transmission or in case of faults stored in the transmission control unit.

  • After change of mecatronics.

  • After change of another component (not mecatronics).

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off - the engine is started during the procedure.

  • Transmission oil temperature between 30°C and 50°C.

  • Vehicle raised so that the wheels can rotate freely.

  • Parking brake applied.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Select the procedure to be performed depending on which job has been done.

  3. Work through the selected procedure.
