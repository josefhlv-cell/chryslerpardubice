Calibration of ABS
This function is used to calibrate adaptive data in the brake system after, e.g., a wheel alignment.
Test conditions:
  • Only cars with DSTC (Dynamic Stability and Traction Control).
  • When replacing control unit or stability sensor.
  • The car stands without load on a level surface.
Procedure:
  1. Brake pedal released.
  2. Ignition on.
  3. Turn the steering wheel at least 15 degrees to the left or right.
  4. Start the calibration.
  5. Run all calibrations in one sequence.

After completed calibration the vehicle shall be test-driven. The drive should be at least 100 metres and include a right and left turn more than 45 degrees, and a brake application from 50 km/h to a standstill. Calibration is approved if the DSTC-system does not turn on any warning light in the combination instrument.
