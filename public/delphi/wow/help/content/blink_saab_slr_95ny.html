[767]Service reset SAAB 9-5 (06->)

This procedure is for clearing service messages on SAAB 9-5 from the year model (06) with (longlife) service intervals. The service intervals depend on the distance covered, the time since the last service, and the driving behaviour. "Time for main/intermediate -service" is displayed in the instrument when it is time for service.

Note:

This procedure is valid for models without info display (SID). If a service is performed between the service intervals (service message not displayed) the longlife parametres must be changed with a diagnostic tool.

Test prerequisites:

  • Valid service performed.

  • "Time for main/intermediate -service" displayed in the instrument.

  • Ignition OFF.

Procedure:

  • Petrol and BioPower.

  1. Ignition ON.

  2. Press the "clear" button on the steering wheel for 10 seconds.

  3. The service reset is confirmed with a sound.

  • Diesel.

  1. Press the brake pedal and hold it down during the procedure.

  2. Ignition ON.

  3. Press the "clear" button on the steering wheel for 10 seconds.

  4. The service reset is confirmed with a sound.
