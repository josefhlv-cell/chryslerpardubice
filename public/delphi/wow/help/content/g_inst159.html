[913] Secondary air system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Battery voltage above 11.5 V.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to ”P/N”.

  • Signals from brake light switch and brake pedal switch OK.

Note:

  • To check the secondary air system.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Secondary air system.

  • Press and hold down the brake pedal. The engine control unit increases the engine speed to about 1400 rpm.

  • The secondary air system diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Let the engine idle until the diagnostic status changes from ”Test on” to ”Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!”.
