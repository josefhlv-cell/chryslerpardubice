[928]Catalytic converter diagnosis

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 60 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the catalytic converter.

  • The diagnosis takes about 100 seconds. If no result is obtained after 150 seconds, change the engine speed within the tolerance range.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Catalytic converter diagnosis.

  • Increase the engine speed to 2800-3200 rpm until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 352 °C.

  • Read the oscillation curve of the catalytic converter. Prescribed value max. 0.55.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from ”Test on” to ”Cat B1 OK! / Cat B1 not OK!”.
