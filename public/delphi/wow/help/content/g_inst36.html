[990] Basic ignition timing signal

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • To check the ignition timing signal.

Procedure:

  • Choose the ignition timing signal function.

  • During basic setting, idling stabilisation is disabled and a fixed ignition point of 12° before TDC is obtained.
