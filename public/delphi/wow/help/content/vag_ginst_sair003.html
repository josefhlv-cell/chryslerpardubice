[1356]Secondary air system diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 40°C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • To check the secondary air system.

  • Under the diagnosis the secondary air pump starts for a few seconds.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Increase the engine speed to 2800 - 3200 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to "Test on".

  • Keep engine speed until the diagnostic status changes from "Test on" to "Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!" (approx. 60s).

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
