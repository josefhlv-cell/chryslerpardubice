[309]Basic setting of pilot injection correction

This function resets teach-in injection correction values and should be run when replacing injector or engine control unit (PCM). After the reset a new teach-in of pilot injection is performed, teach-in takes place at five pressure points in the fuel rail and is repeated a total of three times. Three of the pressure points are taught-in at normal idle rpm and the other two at increased idle rpm.

Test conditions:

  • Engine on.

  • Fuel temperature 25 - 65 °C.

  • Cylinder head temperature 65 - 90 °C.

  • Intake air (IAT) should be between 15 °C - 80 °C.

  • No faults in the system.

  • While basic setting is in progress no controls may be activated.

  • Difference between desired and actual pressure in fuel rail under 10 bar.

  • Difference between desired engine rpm and actual engine rpm under 75 rpm.

  • Requested fuel quantity may not be too high.

  • Idle control should be stable.

  • Deviation values for cylinder balancing should be stable.

  • Idle correction quantity should be stable.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
