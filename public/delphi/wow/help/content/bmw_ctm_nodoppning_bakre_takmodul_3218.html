Emergency opening of rear roof module
This function is used for emergency opening of the rear roof module in case of incorrect or invalid signal from the tailgate lock. The following signals are ignored temporarily by CTM during the function: Tailgate and Luggage compartment separation. Which enables an emergency opening of the rear roof module. Emergency opening is not available to the customer.
Note:
Collision risk! The roof may only be operated when the tailgate is closed.
Test conditions:
  • Place the vehicle on a level surface.
  • The battery voltage should be >12V.
  • Ignition on.

Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
  2. Do the service when the signals to CTM are temporarily turned off and an emergency opening is enabled.
  3. Then finish the function.
