[1087]Motorised throttle position learning

If the control unit or the motorised throttle are replaced the system will perform a self-learn phase for motorised throttle idle. The procedure shall be performed according to the procedure below.

Note:

The accelerator,brake and clutch pedals must not be pressed during this procedure.

Engine must be off.

Performance:

  1. Turn the key to ignition for 60 seconds.

  2. Turn the key to off for 15 seconds.

  3. Turn the key to ignition and check that the parameter “Throttle learn results” has been completed correctly in the data lists.
