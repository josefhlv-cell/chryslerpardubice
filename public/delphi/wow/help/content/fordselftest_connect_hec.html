[842]Self test

Do not activate vehicle switches during the test.

Ensure that following conditions are met:

  • Vehicle is not moving.

  • Switch OFF headlights and heated rear Windshield where installed.

  • Ensure all the doors are closed.

  • Windshield Wipers are OFF and parked.

  • Vehicles with sport/economy switch might need to turn the switch on/off during test.
