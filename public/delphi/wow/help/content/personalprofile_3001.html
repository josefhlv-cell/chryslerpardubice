Personal profile
This function is used to manually change the "Personal profile" settings. The following is personalized via the vehicle information display or combi instrument.
Settings for central locking:
  • Automatic locking after driving off. The vehicle locks automatically when it reaches a speed of 16 km/h. However, it can always be opened from the inside by operating the door's inner lever twice. The first action unlocks the door and the second opens it.
  • Lock again two minutes after unlocking if no door has been opened.
  • Selective central lock. With the first press of the remote control, only the driver's door is unlocked. Only after the second press of the key button is the entire car unlocked.
  • Visible signal upon locking/unlocking.
Note:
In vehicles with M-ASK and CCC, the user programs "Personal profile" into the central information display (CID). In vehicles without M-ASK or CCC, the user programs using the steering column switch for the direction indicator (axial button and rocker switch). Menu navigation is accessed through the combi instrument LCD display.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Ignition on.
Procedure:
Instructions for vehicles with M-ASK and CCC:
  1. Press the MENU button on the information display control unit.
  2. Press the control knob to access the menu.
  3. Turn the control knob to select "Settings". Confirm by pressing the control knob.
  4. Turn to select "Vehicle/Tyres", press to confirm.
  5. Turn to select "Door locks", press to confirm.
  6. A sub-menu is now shown, with either "Central locking" or "Confirmation". Both menus contain settings for central locking. To change sub-menu, move the control knob forwards in the direction of the arrow. Turn to select one of the menus and press to confirm.
  7. The selected menu shows a number of functions. A check mark indicates whether the function is active. Turn to move between the functions and press to activate/deactivate. To go back to the sub-menu in step 6, move the control knob forwards in the direction of the arrow.
Note: If you have navigated incorrectly or wish to cancel, you can always press the MENU button to return to the main menu.
Instructions for vehicles without M-ASK and CCC:
  1. Press the axial BC button on the direction indicator to enter the menu system.
  2. Scroll up or down using the rocker switch on the direction indicator until the "Set" menu, shows a key with a check mark (there are several different "Set" menus).
  3. Confirm by pressing the BC button.
  4. Use the rocker switch to scroll through the functions; then press the BC button to confirm the setting to be changed.
  5. Then perform the setting by using the rocker switch to select and the BC button to confirm.
Note: If you have navigated incorrectly or wish to cancel, wait a moment and the display will automatically return to the main menu.
