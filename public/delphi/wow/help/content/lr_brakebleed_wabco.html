[1636]Bleeding brake system

This basic setting is used when bleeding the anti-lock brake system (ABS). This is only necessary when one of the chambers in the brake fluid reservoir has been empty or when changing parts or brake fluid.

Note:

When bleeding air or changing brake fluid, approx. 2 litres of brake fluid is needed.

Bleeding is performed in a sequence with several steps to be done by the user. Manual bleeding shall be done before this function.

The function "Bleeding modulator" should be done before other bleeding. This should be run until air bubbles stop rising up in the brake fluid reservoir.

Test conditions:

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Correct brake fluid level.

Procedure:

Recommended bleeding sequence: 1. Left front wheel 2. Right rear wheel 3. Right front wheel 4. Left rear wheel

  1. Start the function: "Bleeding modulator".

    1. Follow instructions in the dialogue boxes.

    2. Press down the brake pedal and hold it down.

    3. Press "OK" to continue.

    4. The text "Bleeding in progress" is shown. (Keep the pedal pressed down).

    5. Let up the brake pedal. Press "OK" to continue.

    6. Above steps 1-5 are repeated another 3 times.

    7. A dialogue box shows "Bleeding done".

  2. Select function for each wheel.

  3. Clean the bleed nipples, and connect hoses for bleeding.

    1. Follow instructions in the dialogue boxes.

    2. A dialogue box shows "Pedal shall be let up during the whole sequence".

    3. A dialogue box shows "Pump starts, press OK and open bleed nipple for 6-8 sec. Then close the bleed nipple, the pump stops automatically".

    4. Press "OK".

    5. The text "Bleeding in progress" is shown.

    6. Above steps 1-5 will be repeated another 3 times for the front wheels and 1 time for the rear wheels.

    7. Close bleed nipples.

    8. A dialogue box shows "Bleeding done".

  4. Fill brake fluid.

  5. After finished bleed procedure, check error codes. Take care of any malfunctions, if there are any, and then repeat the bleeding procedure.
