[1716]Change vehicle height

This function is used to check the functioning of the springs.

Note:

check that no people or objects can become trapped under the vehicle and that the wheels are free in the wheel arches during lowering.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Battery voltage above 12 V. Battery charger recommended.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Choose whether to raise or lower the vehicle height.

  3. Use the activation buttons to confirm your choice.

  4. A correct reading can be made approximately 10 seconds after activation.

  5. Stop the function and disconnect the diagnostic tool before working on the vehicle.
