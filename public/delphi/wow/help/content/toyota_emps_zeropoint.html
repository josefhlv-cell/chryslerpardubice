[1230]Torque sensor calibration

After reparation of components in the steering system, initialization of the power steering control module and calibration of the steering wheel center mode must be performed.

This must be done:

  • After replacing the power steering control module .

  • After replacing the steering wheel.

  • After replacing the tilt steering column.

  • After replacing the steering gear assembly.

  • If the fault code C1515 is stored.

Note:

The power steering control module must be correctly initialized before a calibration of the steering wheel torque sensor is performed.

Test prerequisties:

  • The battery voltage should be >12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No stored fault codes except C1515 .

  • Vehicle on a flat and level surface.

  • Tires pointed straight ahead.

  • The steering wheel centered.

Procedure:

  1. Initialize the steering wheel control module by choosing function: Control unit initialization.

  2. A dialog box confirms: operation succeded/failed.

  3. Confirm with OK and exit the diagnosis.

  4. Switch off the ignition without moving the steering wheel.

  5. Switch on the ignition and check that the P/S lamp lit.

  6. Enter the diagnosis and choose function: Torque sensor calibration.

  7. A dialog box confirms: operation succeded/failed.

  8. Confirm with OK and exit the diagnosis.

  9. Switch off the ignition for 3 seconds.

  10. Switch on the ignition and check that the P/S lamp is switched off.

  11. Check and clear fault codes that may occur.

  12. Calibration is complete.

If the calibration failes, check the fault codes and repair possible faults.
