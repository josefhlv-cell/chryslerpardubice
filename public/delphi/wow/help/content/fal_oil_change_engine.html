[135]Reset service information after oil change

This function shall be run after oil change in the engine, when run the parameters related to oil change in the engine control module are updated.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Oil change in engine performed.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Turn off ignition, press "OK".

  3. Wait 30 seconds and turn on the ignition, press "OK".

  4. The function is done.
