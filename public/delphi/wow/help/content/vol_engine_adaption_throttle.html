[702]Throttle adaptation

This function is used to adapt the throttle to the engine control unit.

Note:

Read fault codes and check there are no saved fault codes that could affect and prevent the adaptation.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage at least 10 volts.

  • Accelerator pedal not affected.

  • Vehicle stationary.

  • Engine temperature between 5°C and 100°C.

  • Air intake temperature at least 5°C.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
