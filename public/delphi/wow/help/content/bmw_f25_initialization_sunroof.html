[599]Initiating sliding/elevating sunroof

Note:

Initiation can be run in two ways, manually and via diagnosis.

Crushing protection is not active during initiation!

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Procedure:

Initiating via diagnosis:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

Manual initiation via the control button:

  1. Press the control button in the direction "lift" and hold it there during the entire sequence.

  2. Initiation starts approx. 20-30 seconds after the lifting end-position has been reached.

  3. The inner roof opens (panorama glass roof). The sliding/elevating sunroof is lifted and lowered, then it is opened and closed. The inner roof opens (panorama glass roof) closes.

  4. Initiation is finished when the sunroof and inner roof are completely closed.

  5. Release the control button.
