[1559]Gears, activations

With this function it is possible to activate the different gear positions.

Note:

If a programming fault occurs when activating a gear, the gear cogs in the transmission may not have meshed. Try to move the vehicle a short distance and start the test again.

Test prerequisites:

  • The air pressure in the gearbox shall be 650-1000 kPa.

  • Engine off.

  • Battery voltage higher than 20 V.

Procedure:

  1. Press "OK" to start the function.

  2. Activate selected gear.

  3. At activation a sound should be heard.
