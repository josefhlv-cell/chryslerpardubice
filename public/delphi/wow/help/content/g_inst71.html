[1029]Lambda probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda sond after the catalytic converter.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function. Lambda probe after catalytic converter

  • Maintain the engine speed at 3000 rpm until the exhaust temperature reaches 250 °C. Restore idling. Press and hold down the brake pedal. (Within a few seconds the engine control unit will raise the engine speed to 1400 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read off the value columns. Required result:

Lambda sond (bank 1, sond 2) 0.1 to 0.95V;

diagnosis status must change from ”Test on” to B1-S2 OK!/ B1-S2 not OK!

  • On completion of basic setting, the carbon canister valve is activated automatically.
