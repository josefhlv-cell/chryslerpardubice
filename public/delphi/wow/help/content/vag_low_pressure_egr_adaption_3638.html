Adaptation of low pressure EGR
During basic setting, the low pressure EGR system is adapted to the engine control unit.
Adaption must be done when:
  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.
  • Low pressure EGR valve has been replaced.
  • The engine control unit has been replaced.
Test condition:
  • Engine idling.
  • No faults in the system.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. A dialogue box with current adaptation status is shown.
  3. A flashing indicator light for glowing in the instrument panel, confirms that the adaptation is active.
  4. After completed adaptation, "Adaptation OK" is shown (the adaptation takes approx. 1 minute to perform).
  5. Then follow the instructions presented in the function for ignition on/off.
  6. Press "OK" when the function is complete.
  7. The function is done.
    • If the adaptation fails, "Adaption ERROR" is shown, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
