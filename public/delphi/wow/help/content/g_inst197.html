[955] Catalytic converter diagnosis

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the catalytic converter.

  • Basic setting takes about one minute.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Catalytic converter diagnosis.

  • Increase the engine speed to about 3000 rpm.

  • When the exhaust gas temperature reaches 550 °C, diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Maintain the engine speed until diagnostic status changes from ”Test on” to ”Cat B1 OK! / Cat B1 not OK!”.
