[1620]Dryer agent warning Activation/Deactivation

The function is used to activate or deactivate the built-in system that controls the amount of air that passes through the air dryer and activates a warning when it is time ti change dryer agent reservoir.

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Battery voltage above 20 V.

Procedure:

  1. Select desired function.

  2. A dialogue with test conditions is shown, press "OK".

  3. A dialogue with finish diagnostic function is shown, turn off the ignition and wait 10 seconds.

  4. Turn on the ignition again.

  5. Press "OK" and exit the function.
