[1521]Level sensor, calibration

Note:

Calibration of the level sensor should be performed when:

  • Any of the level sensors has been changed.

  • Work has been performed on the level sensor.

  • The control unit has been changed.

Test prerequisites:

  • The parking brake must be released.

  • Pressure in the primary tank.

  • The operating button on the control box must be in position "MAN1".

  • The wheels must be chocked.

  • The vehicle must be unloaded.

  • Ensure that there is no-one under the vehicle.

  • Check that the control rods are secured between the level sensors and axles.

  • Note: During calibration the vehicle will be raised quickly to check that the level sensors have got the correct polarities.

Procedure:

  1. Make sure that the vehicle is not loaded.

  2. Applies to all trucks with bogies.

    • Put the bogie switch in position 2, centre position.

  3. Adjust any fault codes.

  4. Block the weels and release the parking brake.

  5. Start the engine and fill the system up with compressed air until the compressor releases pressure.

  6. Raise the vehicle with the control box.

  7. Position spacers according to picturers below.

    Front axle

    1. Chassis height-standard

      • 4x2/6x2=330mm

      • 6x4=330mm

      • 6x4/8x4 Rigid, CTN372, VT2014OD/VT2514OD=355mm

    2. Chassis height-900 mm

      • 295mm

    3. Chassis height-850/810 mm

      • 216mm

    Drive axle, generation 1

    1. Chassis height-standard

      • 4x2=337mm

      • 6x2 Pusher, tag=337mm

      • 6x4/8x4 Tractor, SRED-V=337mm

      • 6x4/8x4 Tractor, HRED-V=361mm

      • 6x4/8x4 Rigid CTN372, VT2014OD/VT2514OD=385mm

      • 6x4/8x4 Rigid, other=361mm

    2. Chassis height-900 mm

      • 4x2 Tractor=287mm

      • 6x2 Tractor, pusher=287mm

      • 6x2 Rigid=287mm

    3. Chassis height-850 mm

      • 4x2 Rigid=241mm

      • 6x2 Rigid=241mm

    4. Chassis height-810 mm

      • 4x2 Tractor=241mm

    Drive axle, generation 2

    1. Chassis height-standard

      • 4x2 Tractor=331mm

    2. Chassis height-900 mm

      • 4x2 Tractor=277mm

    • Note: Always place the spacers on both the front axle and the drive axle, irrespective of wich level sensor has been replaced.

  8. Lower the vehicle onto the spacers with the control box, without draining the air bellows.

  9. Calibration shall be performed with the control box operating button in the upper position, "MAN1".

  10. Perform calibration.

  11. Turn off ECS and remove the spacers.
