[174]Reset service message

This function is used to perform a manual service reset.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition off.

  • The ignition is turned on acc. to procedure below.

Procedure:

  1. Insert the key in the ignition.

  2. Press and hold down the button for resetting the trip meter.

  3. During the time that the button is held down, turn on the ignition without starting the engine.

  4. Hold down the button for the trip meter until "SERVICE" flashes on the instrument cluster (approx. 5 seconds).

  5. After another few seconds the message "SERVICE" is lit with a fixed light.

  6. Let up the trip reset button.

  7. Every time the trip meter button is pressed down, 3 messages will be shown for each press of the button, "DIST", "DATE", and "END".

  8. To reset driving distance for service, press the trip meter button repeatedly until "DIST" is shown. To reset time and date for service, press until "DATE" is shown.

  9. When the desired item is selected, press and hold down the trip reset button until "RESET" is shown. Do this for both "DIST" and "DATE" (can also be done only for one of them).

  10. When the reset is performed, cycle until "END" is shown. Press and hold down the button until the display shows present driving distance and time to next service.

  11. Turn off the ignition.
