Battery replacement
When a battery has been installed in the vehicle the function "Register battery replacement" shall be run. If registration of battery replacement does not take place the vehicle's power control will not work correctly. This may lead to malfunctions such as, e.g., reduction or deactivation of individual consumers in the vehicle.
Note:
Should be used to register battery replacement with same capacity.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
