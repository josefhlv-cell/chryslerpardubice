[1288]Catalytic converter diagnosis.

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 440 °C.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the catalytic converter.

  • Test may take several minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Catalytic converter diagnosis.

  • For vehicles to 04.01:

    • Raise engine speed to 2200-2600 rpm.

  • For vehicles from 05.01:

    • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

    • The engine speed will raise by the engine control module.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be above 440 °C.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnostic status field changes from "Test on" to "Cat B1 OK" / "Cat B1 not OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
