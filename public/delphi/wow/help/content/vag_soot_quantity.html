[1386]Resetting of the value for soot quantity in the particle emission filter

It is necessary to reset the soot quantity adaption value when the particle emission filter has been replaced.

Observe:

After resetting of the soot quantity, the ignition shall be turned off for 30 seconds to store the new value.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on and engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Performance:

  • Choose the function: "Content of soot in the particle emission filter".

  • Confirm with OK. A dialog box presents: "Operation succeeded/failed."

  • Confirm with OK to exit the function.

  • Turn off the ignition for 30 seconds to store the new value.
