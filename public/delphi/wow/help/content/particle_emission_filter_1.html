[1107]Cleaning or replacement of particle emission filter

Note:

This function must be used when the particle emission filter is cleaned or replaced. The function resets the parameter for the total quantity of diesel additive that has been injected into the particle emission filter (counter 1).

Performance:

  1. Choose function: Cleaning or replacement of particle emission filter.

  2. A dialog box presents: Operation succeeded/failed.

  3. Press OK.

  4. Check in data list two that the parameter for the total quantity of diesel additive injected in particle emission filter (counter 1) is set to zero.
