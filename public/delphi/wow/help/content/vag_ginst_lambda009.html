[1322]Lambda regulation, catalysator diagnosis

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 440 °C

Note:

  • For diagnosis of catalytic converter efficiency.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda regulation, catalysator diagnosis.

  • Let the engine idle.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • Test may take several minutes.

  • Maintain the idling speed until the diagnostic status field shows "Cat B1 ok /Cat B1 not ok".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
