[86]Calibrate acceleration and yaw rate sensors

Calibration shall be performed if yaw rate sensor, acceleration sensor, or ABS-electronics have been replaced.

Test conditions:

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

  • Ignition on.

Procedure:

  1. Select function "Calibration accelerator and yaw rate sensors", press "OK".

  2. Follow given instructions.

  3. The function is done.
