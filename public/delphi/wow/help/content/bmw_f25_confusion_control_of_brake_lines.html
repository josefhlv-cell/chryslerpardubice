[596]Check for mix-up of brake lines

Note:

The function shall be run when work has been done on the brake lines close to the brake system's hydraulic unit.

Incorrectly installed brake line connections are not identified during normal braking, the vehicle does not become unstable until ABS is activated.

Test conditions:

  • A co-worker is needed to assist with the check.

  • Vehicle raised so that the wheels can rotate freely.

  • Parking brake released.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.
