[660]Adaptation of neutral position sensor.

This function is used for adaptation of the neutral position sensor. After resetting the adaptation value for neutral position sensor has been run, a new adaptation must be run.

Note:

Adaptation requires test-driving of the vehicle.

Test conditions:

To obtain an approved adaptation of the neutral position sensor the vehicle must be driven according to the following scheme 5 times:

  • Every gear must be engaged for at least 5 seconds before changing gears.

  • The clutch pedal has to be let up completely between gearshifts.

  • The gear lever may not be moved between gearshifts.

  • Engine speed must be at least 1200 rpm.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.

  2. After finished function, the following shall be performed:

  3. Shift from 1st gear to 2nd gear.

    Accelerate and then wait for 5 seconds.

  4. Shift from 2nd gear to 3rd gear.

    Accelerate and then wait for 5 seconds.

  5. Shift from 3rd gear to 4th gear.

    Accelerate and then wait for 5 seconds.

  6. Shift from 4th gear to 5th gear.

    Accelerate and then wait for 5 seconds.

    Repeat the shift sequence 5 times.
