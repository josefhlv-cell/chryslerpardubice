[951] Lambda probe heating

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe heating.

  • An implausible value is displayed until the lambda probes have reached operating temperature.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe heating.

  • Read the lambda probe 1, bank 1. Prescribed value: Under 0.9 kohm.

  • Read the lambda probe 2, bank 1. Prescribed value: Under 0.9 kohm.
