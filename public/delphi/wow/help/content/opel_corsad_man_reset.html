[168]Reset service message

This instruction describes how to manually turn off the service light.

Note:

Read through and follow the instructions exactly; if not, the reset system may lock up.

Procedure:

  1. Turn off the ignition.

  2. Press and hold in the reset button (located by the trip meter on the instrument panel).

  3. After 3 seconds "distance to service" is shown on the display "InSP 0".

  4. Press and hold the brake pedal. NOTE! The button still pressed.

  5. Turn on the ignition.

  6. The display shows "InSP----", after the ignition has been turned on, wait until the service message has been changed and hold in the reset button for at least 3 seconds.

  7. Let up the brake pedal and the reset button at the same time.

  8. Wait 10 seconds, until the display shows: "InSP XX,000 km"

  9. The system is reset.
