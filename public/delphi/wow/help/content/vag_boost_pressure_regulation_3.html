[1461]Boost pressure regulation

The basic setting is used to check the boost pressure regulation. During the basic setting the engine speed will be raised to about 1500 rpm, and the boost pressure regulation valve will be opened and closed alternately.

Observe:

It is possible to abort the basic setting by releasing the accelerator/brake pedal.

Test prerequisities:

  • Engine idling.

  • The accelerator pedal and brake pedal should not be operated.

Procedure:

  1. Choose the function: Boost pressure regulation.

  2. Depress and hold the brake pedal when the text "brake" is presented.

  3. Depress and hold the accelerator pedal when the text "full load" is presented.

  4. The engine speed will be raised to about 1500 rpm.

  5. The boost pressure regulation valve will be opened and closed alternately.

  6. Release the accelerator/brake pedal to abort the basic setting.

  7. Exit the function.
