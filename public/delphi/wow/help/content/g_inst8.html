[1038] Throttle control unit

Preconditions for the test:

  • Ignition on.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • The basic setting matches (”adapts”) the throttle control unit to the engine control unit.

Carry out adaption of the throttle control unit:

  • Choose the throttle control unit function.

  • The throttle control unit operates in the min and max positions and three different intermediate positions. The control unit stores the corresponding throttle angle in the permanent memory. After adaption, the throttle returns to the starting position.

  • Quit the basic setting mode.

  • If basic setting is not done, check that the second, third and fifth digits from the right in the block of numerals for setting preconditions are zero.
