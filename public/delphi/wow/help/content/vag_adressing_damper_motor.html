[49]New addressing flap motors

On this car the different flap motors on the climate control unit are activated via a data cable (LIN-BUS). This data cable connects the different flap motors to the control unit. Depending on the repair, one or several basic adjustments have to be performed.

There are the following three basic adjustments for the climate control unit:

  1. "New addressing flap motors"

  2. "Teach-in end-position flap motors"

  3. "Check cooling performance / compressor run-in"

If a new flap motor has been installed or its position has been changed, then basic adjustment 1 and 2 shall be performed in mentioned order.

If a new control unit for the climate control unit has been installed, then basic adjustment 2 and 3 shall be performed in mentioned order.

Note:

  • During addressing, the different flap motors register their positions on the climate control unit.

Test conditions:

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No fault codes stored.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function: "New addressing flap motors", press "OK".

  2. When addressing, "activated" is indicated in the field "Status for the basic setting".

  3. "Completed" is indicated when addressing is done.

  4. Exit the function. Turn the ignition off and then on, wait approx. 15 seconds for any fault codes to be stored.

  5. Re-establish communication and check that no fault codes are stored.

  6. Then continue with the function "Teach-in end-position flap motors".
