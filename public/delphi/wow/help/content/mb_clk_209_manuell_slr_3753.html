Resetting ASSYST after oil change
This function is used to reset the service message.
Test prerequisites:
  • Ignition on, engine off.
Procedure:
  1. Press down the brake pedal and hold it depressed throughout the procedure.
  2. Press the system selection button until the display is in default mode (total and trip meter). This is only necessary if the default mode is not shown.
  3. Press the scroll button (forward/back) until the service symbol and the current remaining distance, remaining time or service exceeded is shown.
  4. Press the reset button on the instrument for about 3 seconds until the request "Press the reset button for 3 seconds" is shown in the multifunction display.
  5. Press the reset button on the instrument within 5 seconds.
  6. The message "Service interval reset" is shown.
  7. The current "remaining distance" in km or miles is shown in the multifunction display.
