[570]Control of glow plug

The test checks that the glow plug works.

Note:

The test can only be started when the fan has stopped.

It may continue for up to 3 min.

During the check, the glow plug is activated for 30 sec.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • Engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The fan's condition is checked.

  3. Press OK to start the check.

  4. The check runs for approx. 30 sec, and then finishes automatically.
