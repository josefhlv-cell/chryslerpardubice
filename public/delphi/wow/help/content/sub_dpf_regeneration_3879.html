Regeneration of particle filter
This function is used to burn soot particles in the particle filter.
Note:
The engine speed is changed during the regeneration. The regeneration time varies according to the amount of soot, it may take more than an hour if it is lots of soot in the particle filter. Stay with the car until the function is complete.
Preconditions for the test:
  • Engine running.
  • Parking brake applied.
Procedure:
  1. Press "OK" to start the function.
