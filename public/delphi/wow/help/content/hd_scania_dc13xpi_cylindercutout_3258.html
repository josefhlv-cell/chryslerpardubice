Shut off cylinder
This function is used to temporarily turn off a cylinder and check the injectors' function.
Test conditions:
  • Engine on.
  • Transmission in neutral.
  • The parking brake must be applied.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. A dialogue box shows the test conditions, check these and press "OK".
  3. Turn off one cylinder at a time, by selecting cylinder and pressing start.
  4. Press stop to activate the cylinder again.
  5. Repeat the test for the other cylinders.
  6. The function is done.
