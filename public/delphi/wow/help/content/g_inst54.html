[1010] Lambda regulation, probe before catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • Used to see the lambda regulation value for the sond before the catalytic converter.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the lambda regulation function.

  • Read off the value columns. Required result: lambda regulation (bank 1, sond 1) –10 % to 10 % (must vary by at least 2%); lambda sond (bank 1, sond 1) 0-1 V (must vary by about 0.3 V).
