[1012] Lambda regulation after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Exhaust temperature above 350 °C.

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda sond after the catalytic converter.

  • During basic setting, the AC compressor is switched off and the carbon canister valve is closed.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: lambda regulation, after catalytic converter.

  • Keep the engine speed between 1800 and 2200 rpm.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Read off the value columns. Required result: lambda sond (bank1, sond2) 0.1 to 0.9 V; lambda regulation, sond before catalytic converter (delay) -150 to 150 ms; diagnosis status must change from ”Test on” to System OK!/System not OK!
