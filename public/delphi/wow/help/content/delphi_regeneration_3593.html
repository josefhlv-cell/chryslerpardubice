Regeneration during driving
At regeneration, the particle filter is cleaned from soot particles by burning them off using an increase in the exhaust temperature. In certain driving conditions, the particle filter can not be cleaned automatically, e.g., in continuous city driving.
This help provides a description of how to perform a regeneration during driving.
Note:
  • It is only necessary to perform a regeneration if the particle filter lamp is on.
  • If the particle filter lamp not goes off after 3 driving procedures, it is probably something wrong with the system.
  • If the particle filter lamp and the engine warning lamp lit simultaneously, exit the regeneration and check for any faults.
  • The exhaust gas temperature becomes very high during regeneration. Park the vehicle on a fireproof surface after the driving until it cools down.
Test conditions:
  • Engine on.
  • Working temperature of engine reached.
Procedure:
  1. Close the program and disconnect the diagnostic tool from the vehicle.
  2. Drive at 3rd gear with an engine speed between 2000-2500 rpm for about 15-20 minutes.
  3. Repeat the procedure until the particle filter lamp goes off.
  4. Regeneration is complete.
    • If the regeneration fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
