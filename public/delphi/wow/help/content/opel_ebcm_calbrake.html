[251]Calibrate brake pressure sensor

This function is used to calibrate the brake pressure sensor.

Calibration of the brake pressure sensor may be needed after certain service jobs:

  • Replacement of electronic brake control module (EBCM).

  • Replacement of electronic brake pressure sensor.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
