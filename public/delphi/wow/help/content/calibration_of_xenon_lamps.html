[776]Calibration of position sensor Xenon lamps

Description:

When ECU for rear electronics (REM) or a position sensor for xenon lamps is changed, calibration is needed.

Caution! If the vehicle is equipped with Four-C, the calibration has to be done in the suspension module (SUM).

A person with a weight of at least 75kg shall sit in the driver seat.

The vehicle shall further be unloaded on a flat surface, handbrake released.

Ignition on.

Erase faultcodes if needed, after the calibration.

Note:

Adjustment of headlight alignment is identical with halogen and Xenon lights.

Ignition on and headlight on.

Make sure that the button for light alignment is in 0-position.

Adjust alignment manually at the head lights.
