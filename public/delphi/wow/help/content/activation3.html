[754] Climatronic, Polo IV

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on

  • Climatronic turned off, (press the button for lower fan speed until ”OFF” is presented in the Climatronic display)

  • For best result should activation be done when a ambient temperature of 12 ° C is presented in the display of the Climatronic

Note:

During “self test” will following components be controlled/activated:

  • Segment of display

  • Test of positioning motors

  • Track to fresh air blower

  • Solenoid

  • Sensors

Procedure:

  • Choose function: ”Self test”

  • Check the function of the components above

  • The “self test” is completed after approximately 30 s. After completed “self test” exit the function and turn of the ignition to reset the system.

  • Read, repair and erase eventual fault codes
