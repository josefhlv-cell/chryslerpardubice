[253]Calibrate steering wheel angle sensor

This function is used to calibrate the steering wheel angle sensor.

Calibration of the steering wheel angle sensor may be needed after certain service jobs:

  • Replacement of electronic brake control module (EBCM).

  • Replacement of steering wheel angle sensor.

  • Replacing steering gear.

  • Replacement of steering column.

  • Collision or other physical damage.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • The steering wheel stands straight and the front wheels point straight forward.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
