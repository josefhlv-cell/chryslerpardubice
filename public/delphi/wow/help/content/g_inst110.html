[861] EGR valve

Preconditions for the test:

  • Ignition on.

  • No faults stored.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • The basic setting adapts the engine control unit to the EGR valve.

Adaption must be done when:

  • The EGR valve or the engine control unit has been replaced.

  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: EGR valve.

  • During adaption, ”Adaption in progress…” shows in the adaption status field.

  • When adaption has been done, "Adaption OK!" / "Adaption Error" is displayed.

Required values at adaption: EGR-valve closed 0.3-1.3 V, EGR-valve open 2.9-4.6 V, EGR valve sensor 0.3-4.6 V.
