[1304]Diagnosis exhaust feedback system

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80°C.

  • Intake air temperature below 60 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

Note:

  • For test of the diagnosis exhaust feedback system.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Diagnosis exhaust feedback system.

  • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

  • The engine speed will raise and the diagnostic status changes from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • Maintain engine speed until the diagnostic status changes from "Test ON" to "Syst. OK! / Syst. not OK!".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
