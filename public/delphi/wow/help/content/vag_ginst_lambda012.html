[1325]Lambda regulation, probe after catalytic converter

Test condition:

  • Engine running

  • No faults stored

  • No leaks in the exhaust system

  • All electrical loads switched off

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C

Note:

  • Used to test the lambda probe after the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Start the function.

  • Depress brake pedal.

  • Raise engine speed to 1500-4000rpm.

  • The status field will change from "Test off" to "Test on".

  • Test may take several minutes.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the status field changes from "Test on" to "B1-S2 OK/B1-S2 not OK".

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
