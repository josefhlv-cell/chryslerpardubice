[873] EGR-system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 70 °C.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the EGR system.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: EGR system.

  • Drive the vehicle for three to four minutes.

  • Keep the engine speed between 1800 and 3600 rpm. Release the throttle pedal quickly to simulate over-revving.

  • The value in the EGR system adaption status column must change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”. Let the engine idle until the value changes to ”System OK!/System not OK!”.

Required values at test: Induction manifold pressure 240-420 mbar; induction manifold pressure difference between open and closed EGR valve at least 50 mbar.
