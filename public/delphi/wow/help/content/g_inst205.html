[964]Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

  • No leaks in the exhaust system.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Automatic gearbox set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • Used to see the ageing factor of the lambda sond (oscillation response). If it is too high, the sond is worn out.

  • The basic setting procedure may take a few minutes.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check

  • Engine speed 2300…2800 rpm.

  • The diagnosis status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter must be at least 350°C.

  • Maintain the engine speed until the diagnosis status field shows ”B1-S1 OK! / B1-S1 not OK!”.
