[832]Fuel leak detection test

Apply reagent to fuel connections that have been removed during the intervention.

Start the engine.

Let the engine run until the coolant temperature is at least 50 °C.

Close the engine hood to avoid fuel projections.

Start the test.

If all conditions are fulfilled the engine revs up.

Wait a few seconds and turn off the ignition.

Open the engine hood and check visually the absence of fuel leak with the reagent previously applied.

Clean the reagent with solvent of type “CARCLEAN”.
