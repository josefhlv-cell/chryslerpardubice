[1625]Calibrating steering angle sensor

This function is used to adapt steering angle sensor to the control unit.

The function shall be performed if:

  • The control unit has been replaced.

  • The steering angle sensor has been disconnected or replaced.

  • Adjustments have been done that may affect the steering angler sensor.

Test conditions:

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • The car shall be at a standstill.

Procedure:

  1. Check that the steering wheel is in central position when the front wheels stand straight.

  2. The car shall be at a standstill with engine off and ignition on.

  3. Select the function: "Steering angle calibration".

  4. Press "OK".

    • A dialogue box with the text "Operation successful" is shown when the calibration is done.

    • If the function failed, check test conditions.
