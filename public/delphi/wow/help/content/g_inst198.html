[956] Secondary air system

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 85 °C.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to P/N.

Note:

  • To check the secondary air system.

  • Basic setting takes about one minute.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Secondary air system.

  • The diagnostic status changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Let the engine idle until the diagnostic status changes from ”Test on” to ”System OK!”. / System not OK!”.
