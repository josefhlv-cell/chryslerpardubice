Reset oil service message
This function resets the service counter for engine oil.
Change engine oil before this function is performed.
Test conditions:
  • Ignition on.
  • All doors/trunk/tailgate and the engine hood should be closed.
Procedure:
  1. Select "Oil service reset" and follow the instructions.
  2. After completed "Oil service reset" do a manual reset of the service lamp.
  3. Follow the instructions below.
  4. Turn on the ignition.
  5. Press down the accelerator pedal and keep it depressed.
  6. Press down the brake pedal 3 times.
  7. Let up the pedals.
  8. Wait approx. 10 seconds.
  9. The display is erased.
  10. Turn off the ignition.
    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
