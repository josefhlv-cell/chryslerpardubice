[1202]Reset fuelling adaption (attempt 1)

If this adjustment fails proceed with Reset fuelling adaption (attempt 2).

Caution: On a vehicle with no fault this procedure is likely to slightly worsen engine emissions.

Reset fuelling adaptions to 100%.

If one or more of the following components have been renewed or rectified the adaption must be performed:

  • Fuel injection or oxygen sensor

  • Fuel pump or fuel rail

  • Inlet manifold pressure sensor

  • Inlet manifold/Air leak repair
