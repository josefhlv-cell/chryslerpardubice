[1373]Induction manifold throttle sensor bank 1, bank 2

The basic setting adapts the engine control unit to the sensor for the induction manifold throttle. An adaption must be done when:

  • The induction manifold throttle sensor has been removed or replaced.

  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.

Test prerequisities:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12 V.

  • No faults stored.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: "Induction manifold throttle sensor for air current flow control, bank 1 or bank 2".

  • During the adaption, "Adaption running" shows in the adaption status field.

  • When the adaption has been performed, "Adaption OK" / "Adaption ERROR" is displayed.

  • The basic setting is finished. Exit the function.

  • Switch off the ignition and wait at least 50 seconds to store the values.
