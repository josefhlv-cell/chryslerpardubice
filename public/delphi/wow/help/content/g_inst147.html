[900] Secondary air system, cylinder bank 1.

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine idling.

  • No faults stored.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Gear selector set to ”P/N”.

Note:

  • For diagnosis of the secondary air system, cylinder bank 1.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Secondary air system, cylinder bank 1.

  • The diagnostic status field will change from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Let the engine idle until the tank ventilation system changes from ”Test on” to ”Syst OK!”. / Syst not OK!”.
