Bleeding the cooling system
This function is used to bleed the coolant circuit after repair.
Note:
The vehicle must stand on level ground.
The entire bleeding process may take up to 25 minutes.
Test conditions:
  • The coolant expansion tank filled to MAX mark.
  • Engine idling.
  • Temperature on "HI".
  • Climate compressor inactive.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function.
  2. Follow the presented instructions for brake, clutch and accelerator.
  3. The bleeding is activated; This is confirmed by the flashing indicator light for glowing.
  4. The first 5 minutes the engine is idling, then it will automatically rev up to about 2500 rpm for about 30 seconds, then to about 3000 rpm before it returns to normal idle.
  5. When the bleeding is complete, the function continues automatically.
  6. In this step, the bleeding is activated by the low temperature circuit, follow the presented instructions. The bleeding may take up to 15 minutes.
  7. After completed bleeding, the coolant level should be checked again.
