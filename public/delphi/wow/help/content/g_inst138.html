[890]Kick-down

Preconditions for the test:

  • Ignition on.

  • No faults stored.

Note:

  • Used when teaching kick-down position to vehicle with automatic transmission.

Adaption must be done when:

  • The engine control unit has been removed from the vehicle.

  • The throttle pedal has been replaced.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Kick-down.

  • The adaption status field will change to ”Activate kick-down”.

  • Press the throttle pedal past the kick-down position and keep it there for at least two seconds.

  • Read adaption status. Prescribed value Must change from ”Activate kick-down…” to ”Adaption OK!” / Adaption Error!”.
