Check the oil level in the transmission
This function is used to check the oil level in the transmission.
Note:
  • If the oil cooler or the thermostat has been replaced, the transmission oil temperature must be heated to at least 90°C for 5 minutes to fill the oil cooler with oil. When the oil temperature has cooled to below 35°C, the function for checking the transmission oil may be performed.
Test conditions:
  • Engine idling.
  • Transmission oil temperature as specified in the function.
  • Position the car on a flat and level surface.
  • Gear selector in position P.
  • No faults in the system.
  • All electrical loads should be switched off.
  • ESP disconnected.
  • The engine skid plate removed.
Procedure:
  1. Start the function and follow the instructions in the program.
