[1365]Throttle control unit (current lower mechanical stop)

The current lower mechanical stop changes with the engine´s operation/ wear. An adaption of this must be done when:

  • An adaption of the throttle control unit (original lower mechanical stop) has been performed.

  • The throttle control unit has been removed.

  • The electric power to the engine control unit has been disconnected.

Test prerequisites:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • The battery voltage should be above 11.5 V.

  • The throttle pedal should be inactive when the adaption is performed.

  • The intake air temperature should be at least 6 °C.

Procedure:

  • Chose the function: Throttle control unit (current lower mechanical stop).

  • During the adaption, "Adaption running" shows in the adaption status field.

  • When the adaption has been performed, "Adaption OK" / "Adaption ERROR" is displayed.

  • The adaption is finished. Exit the function.

  • Switch off the ignition and wait at least 50 seconds to store the values.
