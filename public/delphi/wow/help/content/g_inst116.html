[866] Knock sensor

Preconditions for the test:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • Engine temperature above 80 °C.

Note:

  • For testing knock sensors.

  • The tests are done during a test drive.

Procedure:

  • Choose the function: Knock regulation.

  • Keep the engine speed above 2200 rpm and load the motor with more than 15 %.

  • The diagnostic status field changes from ”Test off” to ”Test on”.

  • Vary the engine speed between 2200 and 5700 rpm and load the engine by 15-100 %, until the diagnostic status field changes to ”Sys. OK/ Sys. not OK!”.
