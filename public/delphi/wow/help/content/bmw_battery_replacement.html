[2]Battery replacement

This function is used when a new battery has been installed in the vehicle. If registration of battery replacement does not take place the vehicle's power control will not work correctly. This may lead to malfunctions such as, e.g., reduction or deactivation of individual consumers in the vehicle.

Primarily, a battery with the same battery capacity should be installed in the vehicle. If a battery with higher or lower battery capacity or an AGM-battery is installed, the vehicle's coding data must be changed.

There are two types of batteries:

  • Normal lead battery (recognized by the white casing).

  • AGM-battery (recognized by the black casing).

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery charger connected to vehicle.

  • Battery replaced.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The presently registered battery capacity is shown, press "OK".

  3. If a battery with higher or lower capacity or an AGM-battery is installed, press "YES". If a battery with the same capacity as before is installed, press "NO" and go to step 5.

  4. In the list, select the battery that has been installed, press "OK". If battery capacity of the installed battery is not in the list, select the one closest to it.

  5. Press "OK" to register battery replacement.

  6. The presently registered battery capacity is shown and the odometer reading for the four latest registered battery replacements. Press "OK" to exit the function.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
