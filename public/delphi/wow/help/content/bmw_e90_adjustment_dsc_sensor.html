[620]Adjusting DSC-sensor

This function is used after changing DSC-sensor. This process adjusts the lateral acceleration sensor.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on.

  • The vehicle must stand on level ground.

  • Pedals may not be activated during the function.

  • No faults in the system.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the program.

  3. The function is done.
