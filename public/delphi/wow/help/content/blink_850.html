[1406] Cancel service lamp 850 m/92-97, Yasaki

850 m/92-97 with Yasaki instrumentation is cancelled manually . Remove the rubber plug in the instrument glass at the trip meter and press the button behind it.

The difference between Yasaki and VDO instruments

The VDO instrument panel is completely flat

The Yasaki instrument panel has recessed instruments

Note:

850 m/92-95 with VDO instrumentation, service lamp is cancelled with the instrument by the 6-pin diagnostic socket, connection A7 .

850 m/96-97 with VDO instrumentation, service lamp is cancelled with the instrument by the OBD II -socket.
