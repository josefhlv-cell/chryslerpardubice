[1612]Reset the adaptation values for particle filter

When replacing particle filter, the adaptation values for calculated soot quantity and soot volume must be reset in the engine control unit.

Note:

After resetting soot quantity, the ignition shall be turned off for 30 seconds to store the new value.

Test conditions:

  • Battery voltage at least 12 V.

  • Ignition on, engine off.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function: "Replacing particle filter".

  2. Confirm with "OK", reset in progress.

  3. Calculated soot quantity + soot volume are shown after performed function, these values shall be zero after a successful reset.

  4. Exit the function with "OK".

  5. Turn off the ignition for 30 seconds to store the new values.
