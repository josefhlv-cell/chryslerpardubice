[231]Bleed air from fuel system

If the high-pressure pump or components in the fuel system have been replaced or removed it is necessary to bleed air from the fuel system.

  • Air bleeding shall take place on a closed system.

  • Further instructions are shown during the bleeding sequence.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Follow the instructions in the programme.

    • Bleding can be cancelled at any time during the function.

  3. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
