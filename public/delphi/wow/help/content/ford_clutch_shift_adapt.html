[640]Adaptation of clutch system.

Run this process if a new clutch or a new transmission control unit has been installed.

Note:

It takes approx. 5 minutes to complete the teach-in. There must be free space around the car as it may jerk when checking the transmission function.

Test conditions:

  • Engine idling.

  • Battery voltage between 12-15V.

  • The foot brake is affected so that a pressure of more than 15 bar is reached.

  • Air conditioning is off.

  • Gear selector in position [P].

  • When instructed, the accelerator pedal shall be pressed down fully and held down.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function. Follow the instructions in the program.

    • If the engine speed remains above 4,000 rpm for more than 5 seconds the function shall be interrupted by letting up the accelerator pedal.

    • The engine speed will vary during the process.

  2. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
