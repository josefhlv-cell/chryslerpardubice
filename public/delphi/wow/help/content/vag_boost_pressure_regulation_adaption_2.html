[287]Basic setting of boost pressure regulator

In the following basic setting the boost pressure regulator is operated to both of its end-positions. These are registered and saved in the engine's control unit. Adaptation should be performed when:

  • The boost pressure regulator has been replaced or has been removed.

Test conditions:

  • Ignition on, engine off.

  • No faults in the system.

  • Coolant temperature below 45 °C.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. The adaptation starts, this is indicated by "Adaptation in progress".

  3. When adaptation is finished, "Adaptation OK" is shown.

    • If "Adaptation Error" is shown, check that test conditions are fulfilled and repeat the function.

  4. Press "OK" when the function is complete.

  5. Check that no error codes are stored in the engine control unit.

  6. The function is done.
