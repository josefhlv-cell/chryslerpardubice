[1345]Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check

Test condition:

  • Engine running.

  • No faults stored.

  • All electrical loads switched off.

  • Coolant temperature at least 80 °C.

  • Induction air temperature below 60 °C.

  • The temperature of the catalytic converter at least 352 °C.

Note:

  • Used to do an age check of the lambda probe before the catalytic converter.

Procedure:

  • Start the function: Lambda probe before catalytic converter, age check.

  • Depress brake- and throttle pedal.

  • The engine speed will raise and the status field will change from "Test OFF" to "Test ON".

  • Check period. Target value: max 2.2 s.

  • Maintain raised engine speed until the status field changes from "Test ON" to "B1-S1 OK" / "B1-S1 not OK".

  • Test may take several minutes.

  • If the specifications are not attained, interrogate fault memory.

  • Exit function.
