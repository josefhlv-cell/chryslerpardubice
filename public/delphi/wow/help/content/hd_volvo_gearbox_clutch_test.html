[1614]Test of clutch wear check

The check is used to read out the value for a new clutch (X1-value), remaining thickness of clutch disc, and upper and lower level for changing clutch disc.

Test conditions:

  • Parking brake applied.

  • Air pressure in transmission between 550-1,000 kPa.

  • Engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 20 V.

  • Oil temperature in transmission above 15°C.

Procedure:

  1. Select the function: "Clutch wear check, test".

  2. A dialogue with test conditions is shown, press "OK".

  3. A dialogue with values from the wear check is shown.

  4. Press "OK" and exit the function.
