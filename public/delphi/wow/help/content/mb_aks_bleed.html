[203]Bleed the clutch system

This function is used to bleed the clutch when needed. Battery charger must be connected during the entire sequence. The system may not be pressurized with a bleeder unit during bleeding, brake fluid may only be filled manually.

Test conditions:

  • Engine off.

  • Battery voltage above 12V.

  • Empty container connected on bleed nipple.

  • Make sure that the vehicle cannot start to roll with the gear in neutral.

Procedure:

  1. Start the function.

  2. Bleeding is divided into three steps where dialogue boxes appear to guide you through the entire sequence.

    Brief explanation of the three steps:

    1. Draining the system: The clutch will be drained of brake fluid, do not fill more brake fluid at this point.

    2. Filling the system: New brake fluid should be filled in the clutch, during this step the brake fluid reservoir may not be empty at any time.

    3. Bleeding is finished: After finished bleeding it is important to check the function of the clutch with a test-drive.

  3. A dialogue box shows if the operation was successful or failed when bleeding is finished.

  4. The function is done.

    • If the function fails, check the test conditions and repair any faults.
